# Source provenance

Manuscript images were accessed with the DECODE project's permission; images are not redistributed. The manifest preserves image identifiers and checksums for comparison by authorized readers, without local filesystem paths.

`R1889-DECODE.txt` and `R1890-DECODE.txt` are the DECODE attachments transcribed by XZ in January 2021; `R953-DECODE-rejected-comparison.txt` is AP's May 2020 key transcription. They are retained byte-for-byte, with their original attribution and uncertainty marks, to make the derived transcriptions reproducible. They are not our original transcriptions or decipherments. Direct source links, record identifiers and qualifications are in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md).

The working layers in `../transcriptions/` identify our normalization, image checks, and review amendments. No attachment or manuscript image is represented as an independently validated plaintext witness for 1640.
