# The separate 1634 cipher

The letter of 28 October 1634 (DECODE R1887) has a different graphic and syllabic repertoire from the 1635/1640 correspondence. Its [partial decipherment and source-aligned apparatus](../ferdinand-1634/README.md) are published alongside those letters on the [Ferdinand correspondence page](https://aaymeloglu.github.io/unsolved-ciphers/ferdinand-reading.html#letter-1634).

No matching key sheet or corresponding plaintext draft was used. The catalogue suggestion of a draft dated 16 November 1634 is not a verified witness: the known R954 draft is dated 16 November 1635 and uses the later repertoire. Values from the 1635/1640 key are not imported into 1634. The source, uncertainty and verification records are in the separate 1634 folder.
