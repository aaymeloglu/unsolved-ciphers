## A recurring form: long stroke plus arch ("f r" vs "h")

This page has a sign made of one long stroke, with a hooked top above x-height and a foot well
below the baseline, plus an arch or r-shoulder at x-height starting from the stroke. Where the
stroke runs straight below the baseline I read it as long `f` followed by `r`, because the r's shoulder is
separate from the stroke (L15 x~150, L17 x~420, 1270, 2100, 2680). Two cases differ: the stroke
**loops back up** from below the baseline into the arch (L16 x~1440, x~2080). The c. 96 `h`
(h_1, h_2) has no such loop and does not descend, so these two are `{h/f}`. If they are `f`, the arch after the loop is
probably a separate `r`, so the token count would grow by one at each.
