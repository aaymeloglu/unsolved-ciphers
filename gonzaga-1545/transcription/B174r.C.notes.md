# B174r third reader (C) notes

Method: own crops from the prepared page (rotate -0.25 via cipherkit.transcribe.prepare), 5-9x at every open spot, with neighbours for scale. x = page pixels. Decided by shape only.

Two conventions used throughout:
- **h second form vs f + n / f r.** Where a long sloping stem that descends below the baseline carries a minim branching from the stem itself, then an arch (with or without a full leg), I read the second form of h, by comparison with L8 x~2440 and L9 x~910, which both readers took as `h r`. I read `f n` only where the stem stands free of the following minims.
- **c with a dot or stroke directly above it** is read as the accented e. A plain open c with no tick or mark is c.
- **looped d-shaped signs.** I wrote `y` where the ascender is heavy and joined to the bowl, `d` where it is thin, near-vertical and free of the bowl or nearly so. Where the ascender is a short diagonal rising from the bowl's own stroke, as in the final-e form (glyphs e-final_1), the sign is left as `{y/e}` or `{d/e}`.
