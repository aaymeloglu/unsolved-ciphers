Two working rules for this page, used at many spots:

- **h**: this hand's h (exemplar `h_1`) is an ascender over a *one*-legged body. A long sloping
  stem with an r-like arch whose foot stops at or just under the baseline (no crossbar) is one sign,
  h (second form). Only where the stem runs clearly below the baseline (20+ px) is it `f` plus the
  following sign. A second full leg after an h body is a further sign.

  minim. That rising stroke is not a separate n/a (L7, L12).
