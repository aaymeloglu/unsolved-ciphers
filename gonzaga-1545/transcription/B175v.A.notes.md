## Rules I applied to this page

- **g / z**: closed lower loop = `g`; 9-bowl with an open tail sweeping left to a hook = `z`.
  Both `g` and `q` often have a dark, peaked (angular) bowl; I did not count the peak as a
  separate sign. Places where a reader might see an extra `a`/`n` before the descender sign:
  L7 x~560 (`p n q b`), L12 x~1900 (`r b q r`), L18 x~560 (`f n g r`), L21 x~1480 (`n q b`),
  L24 x~1650 (`g r q r`).
- **h**: two forms. The usual one is the 5-like form of the exemplars. A second is a long sloping
  stem with an r-like arch, which can be taken for `f r`; where the stem clearly goes below the
  baseline I wrote `f`, otherwise `h`. L6 end `f h h b` and L23 x~1520 `{f/h} r` depend on this.
- **t** (cross form, standing on the baseline, no descender) is frequent on this page: L4 x~1000,
  1280, 1560, 2010; L15 x~2050; L17 x~880; L18 x~1620; L19 x~900; L20 x~1790, 2080; L21 x~900,
  1400, 2000; L23 x~560, 1700, 1960.
- **19** is written as a short minim plus a 9 with a long tail (L4 `b 19 b`, `x 19 r`, and often).
- A bowl-less e with a dot or tick above is written `e` (accented form); listed per line below.
