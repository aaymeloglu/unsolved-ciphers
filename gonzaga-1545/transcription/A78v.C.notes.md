# A78v third reader (C): adjudication notes

Settled from the image only (prepared page, rotate -0.75, layout bands), every spot cut at 4-8x, with
neighbouring plain signs of the same family for comparison. x positions are processed-page pixels.
No decoding.

Working rules applied across the page (decided once on clear examples, then checked per spot):

- **Long-form h vs f + sign.** A long stem with no crossbar crossing it, from whose middle an arch
  or r-like arm springs, is the long-form `h` of glyphs.md section 6 (L3 750, L5 600). The f's of
  this page (L19 1850, L22 2000, L24 2260) have a bar that crosses the stem. Descender depth does
  not separate them here: the plain f's descend only ~23-27 px, the long h's ~10-20 px.
- **Plain h has two downstrokes** (stem + one arch-and-leg: L3 900, L22 2050), and "h n" where both
  readers agree has four (L6 650, L7 2330). A cluster of three downstrokes is therefore h plus a
  one-downstroke sign: where the third stroke has a lead-in from the top of the h's leg and a
  shoulder at its top running right into the next sign, it is the r of this hand (cf. the plain
  r's of L6 760-900), so `h r`.
- **Gaps** `|`: marked only where the x-height band (column projection, 38-72% of the band) has a
  blank run clearly wider than the 15-20 px of ordinary letter spacing.
- **s vs 5**: on this page the 5 of 53 is identical in shape to the short round s (L1 2290,
  L3 1350, L12 1240-1480, L24 2370), so agreed `{s/5}` stays open unless the sign stands tight
  against a digit as part of a group.
