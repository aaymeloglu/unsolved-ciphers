## Page level

- **Lines.** 29 manuscript lines, matching the 29 layout bands one to one. No merged or split lines.
- **Method.** I read each line from my own strips, cut from the same layout bands with the same `prepare` call (rotate -2.25) and padded 40 px above and 50 px below.
  - Lines 2-29 were read in five overlapping segments at 2.6-2.8x. Line 1 was read in three segments at 1.8x, plus enlargements.
  - I did not open the `halves/` PNGs themselves; my strips cover the same bands.
  - Every sign that was not plain I enlarged at 3.5-9x. Per-line enlargements are listed below.
- **d/y review.** This was done after the line-by-line pass and supersedes the per-line `{d/y}` / `{y/d}` notes below.
  - All 42 bowl-plus-ascender signs left as pairs were set side by side at 3.5x with my plain y (L5 1520, L6 1030, L6 2120) and plain d (L2 1790, L23 2110, L26 2390).
  - I applied the glyphs.md rule: a heavy closed bowl with the ascender rising from the bowl and leaning back, often with a foot hairline, is y; an open c-bowl with a thin separate upright is d.
  - 32 are now plain `y`.
  - Still `{y/d}`: L10 1520, L12 990, L14 2500, L16 2440 (top touched by line 15's z tail), L17 980 (blotted), L23 1235 (blotted), L29 870, L29 2120 (faint).
  - Still `{d/y}`: L23 1700 and L27 1080 (open bowl, thin upright).
  - Plain `d` stays at L2 1790, L2 2445, L7 1000, L23 2110 and L26 2390.
- **Wide gaps `|`.** None written.
  - I measured blank column runs in the x-height zone of every band. The threshold was 45 px: about 9x the median inter-sign gap of 5 px, and above the 98th percentile (34 px).
  - Apart from line ends at the right margin, only three gaps pass: L10 x~2047 (46 px), L26 x~883 (65 px) and L26 x~957 (46 px).
  - The L26 gaps are the open space around the long sloping h. L10's is barely over the threshold.
  - None is clearly wider than letter spacing.
- **c/e coverage.** Lines 1-8 had a 7x montage of every open c-shape. In lines 9-29 only the c-shapes I flagged while reading were enlarged; unflagged plain `c` there were read at 2.6-2.8x only.
- **Show-through.** The verso writing shows through heavily as light-brown mirrored letters:
  - all down the left margin;
  - between the lines across most of the page;
  - most strongly behind lines 13-14 (x~450-1400) and around lines 17-22.
  
  None of it is transcribed.
- **Left margin.** One dark mark sits in the left margin between lines 4 and 5 (y~860, x~250-420): a horizontal stroke with short vertical hatches across it. It is a pen flourish or a struck-out marginal word, not part of the cipher line, and is not transcribed. Nothing else in the margin is original ink.
- **Brown ink and blots.** The page has many brown ink spots and smears, and several blots. They are noted per line where they sit on or next to a sign.
- **Overwritten passages.** A few signs are written over lighter, earlier strokes: L4 x~1540, L5 x~1630-1860, L9 x~1235-1410. I transcribed only the final black signs.
- **Punctuation.** Colon-like double dots appear at L19 line end (x~2710) and L24 x~1800. A short baseline dash appears at L26 x~2315. None of these is transcribed.
- **h, second form.** The sloping h of glyphs 6 is frequent: a long stem from below the baseline with an r-like arch. I write it `h` everywhere. A reader could write it `f r` or `f n`. The places are listed per line.
