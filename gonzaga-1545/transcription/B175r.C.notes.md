# B175r, third reader (C): settling the open spots of B175r.AB.txt

Method: every spot was cut from the prepared page (rotate -0.25, layout bands) at 3.5-8x with neighbours, and where a family of signs was at issue, set side by side with agreed instances of each candidate from this page. Decided by shape only; nothing was decoded. x positions are prepared-page pixels, good to about +-20.

Rules applied across the page (each calibrated on signs both passes agree on):

- **`|` gaps.** I measured the blank run in each line's x-height zone with one script. I calibrated on the agreed `|` at L2 x~1510 (27 px) and set the threshold at 26 px or more. The other agreed `|` (L7 x~618) measures only 18 px, so `|` stays a weak signal.
  - Plain stretches that both passes left unmarked also measure 26 px or more: L7 x~848 (31), L8 x~2620 (26, probably the s's exit knot), L10 x~1320 (26), L11 x~2603 (32). I did not add `|` there.
- **Barless f vs h (second form).** The agreed f's on this page often show no bar left of the stem. Their "bar" is the join into the next sign (L20 x~2560, L22 x~2040, L19 x~2600), and their thin stem runs well below the baseline. The agreed h second form (L20 x~1225) starts at x-height and does not descend.
  - So a thin stroke from well below the baseline to a hooked top, followed by an r or n with its own downstroke, is read `f r` / `f n`. This covers L6, L8, L9, L12 (twice), L15, L16, L17, L22 (twice) and L23.
  - If the h second form can in fact descend, all of these become `h`. That is the main systematic risk on the page.
- **h plus flag.** Where an h's right leg rises again into a heavy, clubbed flag at x-height, I read an r joined to the h: L3, L21, L23, L25. Pass A reads r at all four, pass B at none. An h's own body ends on the baseline.
- **z vs g.** The agreed g closes a full oval loop under its bowl (L20 x~1400, L13 x~2300), or takes the open-u "modern y" form (L24 x~960). The agreed z (L13 x~445) has a tail that sweeps down-left and may curl up, but stays open.
- **c vs e.** I read e only where an eye, an inner tick or an accent is visible at 6-8x. Otherwise the spot is `{c/e}`, as glyphs.md says.
- **19.** A narrow minim with its foot turned right, then an open 9 with a long tail sweeping left under it, matches the agreed 19 at L9 x~1230 and L3 x~1730.
