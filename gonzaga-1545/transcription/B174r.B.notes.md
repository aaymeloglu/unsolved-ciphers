Shape rules applied throughout:
- **h vs f + n**: h = tall stem whose r-/n-like arch springs directly from the stem (stem may dip below
  baseline in the sloping second form). f + n = long-descender stem standing free, with the following
  minims separate from it (a gap or hairline join only at the foot). Cases: L2 x~760 h; L2 x~1700 f n;
  L3 x~1130 f (before c); L3 x~1230 h.
- **h (first form) vs b**: ascender with an open, 5-like body = h; ascender with a closed 6-like body = b.
- `|` written only where the gap is clearly wider than normal word spacing.
