# B175v, pass B: notes

Lines: 26, matching BRIEF.md. The layout bands match the manuscript lines one to one; nothing is merged or split.

Method: I read each line from my own 2.4-2.7x crops of the rotated page (`processing.rotate` 0.25), cut into five segments with x ticks. I also made 4-6x enlargements at every doubtful spot. All x positions below are in source pixels.

Doubtful signs: **64 `{a/b}` tokens, and no `?`**, out of about 1,260 signs (about 5%). Most of them are of four kinds:
- c vs e: open c-shapes where I cannot see the e's tick.
- short round s vs bare 5 where no digit stands next to it (written `{s/5}` as glyphs.md section 6 says).
- a vs n.
- o vs b: a small round body whose ascender is faint or missing.
