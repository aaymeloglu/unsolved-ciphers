## Conventions I applied

- 9-shape with a minim directly before it: `19`. 9-shape alone with its tail running to a blob, no loop: `z` or `{z/g}`. Descender ending in an open hook without a closed loop: `{g/z}`. Closed lower loop: `g`.
- Bowl plus ascender: heavy, continuous, leaning: `y`; thin, upright, detached or dotted: `{d/y}`; unclear: `{y/d}`.
- f plus arch written in one movement is `f r` when the arch has one leg and `f n` when it has two; the two are close.
- c without a visible tick is `c`; with a hooked or thickened top `{c/e}` or `{e/c}`; e with a separate tick or dot above is `e` (accented form) and is noted, since it can be read as i.
- `|` only at L1 (after 24, after `58 x y`), L4 (x~1040) and L8 (x~860), where the gap is clearly wider than letter spacing. Smaller gaps are mentioned in the line notes only.
