  loses one token there (`{a/u/x} g` becomes `19`).

  `c`, `r` after `h`) are not on the page; each place is one sign, so the line is one token shorter
  than the longer pass.

  7s: it has a heavy stroke along the baseline (a Z) and its diagonal runs on below the line to a
  dot. It is a 7 with a bar or a 2 with a tail; shape does not settle it (pass B: `{22/27}`).
- L7 1915: the merge's plain `z` after `53` has the same form as the 9 in the open spot at 1345;
  not changed.
- Recurring forms used in the decisions above: (1) h is a stem plus one arch-leg, in both its
  short form (agreed L2 1660) and its long sloping form that dips below the baseline (agreed plain
  h at L7 2600). Checked on one sheet at one zoom: stem + 1 leg = h (L7 2395, L9 1145, L12 2030);
  stem + 2 legs, the second flagged = h r (L2 1660 agreed, L3 690 agreed, L3 1050, L6 835,
  L10 2350); L11 1650 has 2 legs but no flag (left `{r/n}`). (1b) This hand's h also takes an
  open 5-like body curling in at the foot (L1 1215, L15 1060, L4 2560); b has a closed bowl. (2) The pointed n
  is an inverted V without a bowl; an a has a closed bowl, which in heavy ink is filled solid or
  shows only a slit (L3 2595, L4 1620, L13 1920). (3) "e" + dot + descender sign at L2 2000,
  L5 700 and L10 1530 is e (c-shape with tick or eye) followed by g or z; the dot is the e's. The
  1 of this page's agreed 19s (L9 900, L13 740) is a free-standing upright minim, undotted and
  hooked, standing apart from the 9; in these three places the stroke before the descender sign is
  a curved c with a tick, joined to the preceding letter, and there is no upright minim.
- p-groups: L4 2560-2700 `p n a`, L9 1900-2030 `p r n`, L15 1080-1170 `p n` were put side by side;
