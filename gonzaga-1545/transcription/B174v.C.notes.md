## Rule I used for the long-stem signs (`f r` / `f n` / `h`)

The page has many signs made of a long sloping stem, a hooked top and an arch. I read them like this:
- no crossbar and one arch-leg after the stem: `h` (the second h form of glyphs.md section 6);
- a stem that carries a full two-legged n after it: `f n`;
- a visible crossbar into a separate r: `f r`.

Under this rule, L14 x530, L16 x840, L17 x620/1270/2100, L18 x1460, L20 x450 and L22 x1580 are all `h`, and L21 x1770 is `f n`. A later reader who prefers `f r` for the one-leg form should change all of them together.
