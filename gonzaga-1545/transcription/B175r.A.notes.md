**Lines 1-12 are by an earlier reader of this pass who was interrupted; they have no notes and were left exactly as found.** The file held 12 lines when I took over, not 11. I checked line 11 against `halves/B175r/B175r-L11a.png` and line 12 against `B175r-L12a/b.png`: both are the right manuscript lines, complete to the line end. Lines 13-26 are mine.

Layout: at the far left of the strips (left of the text block) there are stray signs from the facing page (for example beside lines 13-17 and 20-25); they are not line starts and are not transcribed. 26 bands, one real line each; nothing merged or split. Line 26 is a full-width line, not a tail. x positions below are source pixels in the prepared page (rotate -0.25), good to about ±30.

Method for 13-26: first reading from five overlapping 3x tiles per line cut from the prepared page, with 5x-8x crops of every sign whose shape was not plain; then every half strip (13a-26b) read against the result for sign order and count. The strip pass changed one sign (L16 x~760, b to h). No decoding was done.

`|` gaps: the scribe's gaps are small. I measured blank runs in the x-height zone: the earlier reader's three `|` (lines 1, 2, 7) sit on runs of 29-35 px, against a usual sign pitch of about 45 px. No interlinear insertions in 13-26. No sign that I can call struck out; two heavy or blotted places are doubtful (L23 x~2200, L25 x~490, see below).

Show-through from the verso is visible between most lines as pale brown shapes; it never sits on a sign so as to change a reading, but it is behind the accents of line 26 x~1000-1100.
