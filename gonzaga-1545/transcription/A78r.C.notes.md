# A78r third reader (C): adjudication notes

Settled from the image only: prepared page (rotate -2.25, layout bands), each spot cut at 4-10x with
plain neighbours for scale, contrast-stretched where the ink is pale. x positions are processed-page
pixels (approximate). No decoding.

Working rules, applied the same way everywhere:

- **Long sloping stem from below the baseline.** No crossbar and one arch-leg: `h`. Stem carrying a
  full two-legged n: `f n`. A real crossbar into a separate r: `f r`.
- **Lone minim + open 9-shape with a long tail curling left:** `19` (glyphs.md section 6). This
  settles all of A's `{1/i} {g/z}` pairs: L19 1205, L21 1960, L21 2330, L22 720, L22 2200,
  L26 2260, L28 1540.
- **9-shape alone, single tail down-left, no closed loop:** `z`. **Straight descender ending blunt
  or in a small hook:** `q`.
- **Bowl + ascender.** Ascender growing out of the bowl, often with a foot hairline: `y`. Open
  c-bowl with a thin upright standing apart: `d`.
- **Gaps** `|`: none written. None of A's gaps (L1 x2, L4, L8) is clearly wider than the letter
  spacing of the same line at enlargement.
