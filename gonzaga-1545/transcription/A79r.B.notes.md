# A79r pass B notes

Layout: 15 bands = 15 manuscript lines; no merges or splits. Read from my own crops of the page rotated -1.75 deg (layout bands), at 2.2x for each quarter-line, then 4-9x on every sign that was not plain. x positions below are page coordinates of the rotated image.

Doubtful signs: 18 `{a/b}` tokens on the page (about 1.6% of ~1130 signs). No `?`. No struck-out signs and no caret insertions seen.

## Per line

- **L1**: All plain at 4-6x. The `L` signs at x~1530 and ~2560 are full-size bare L.
- **L2**: Starts `4o 3o`. The 4 is open with a single crossbar, followed by a ring, so I read it as 4o and not qx. x~1100-1260 was enlarged 6x: `t y x r t`. The dotted sign at x~2000 (9x) is cipher x, not a 1. `14` is at x~1830 and `18` at x~2100.
- **L3**: `L` full-size at x~1010 and ~1300. `q x` at ~1330 is ordinary q and x, not the 4-ligature. The line ends with full-size `53`.
- **L4**:
  - x~1370-1490 (8x) is a three-minim **m-shape**, transcribed `m`. Cipher m is not in glyphs.md section 1, so it should be looked at again. It is not `a n` or `n a`: the minims are joined at the top, and there is no bowl.
  - `8` at x~1500 is a bare 8, standing alone and not after a 1.
  - x~2620 `{d/e}`: an e-like bowl with a tall loop rising from its exit. This could be the final-e form or a d.
- **L5**:
  - x~720 `{n/a}`: a half-closed bowl.
  - x~1500 `3o` followed by `o`: there is a second, separate full-size ring after the 3o ring (5x). I transcribed it as cipher `o` and not as a digit.
  - x~2140 `{s/5}`, followed by `27`.
- **L6**:
  - Starts `L 53` with both full-size.
  - x~1940 `{e/c}`: a faint inner tick.
  - x~2190 `{t/f}`: a long sloping stroke with a crossbar and a looped descender. It is between t and f; the descender loop may belong to the next g.
  - x~2680 `{c/e}`: an open c with a stroke running into the x. No eye is visible at 8x.
- **L7**:
  - x~860-1000 `y h n` is very heavy (a blot). x~1040-1140 was read at 9x as `h n`, with h in the long-stem form.
  - x~1290 `{s/5}`, then x~1320 `{z/9}`: a 9-shape with a long tail curling left. It is next to the s/5, so it could be a digit group 59 or s + z.
  - x~1890 `{9/z}`: right after full-size `53`.
  - x~2520 `{d/e}`: same shape as L4.
  - The line ends `L h x`. The h x pair at x~2620-2700 has its strokes crossing, and I read it as two signs.
- **L8**: The faint brown ink at x~1700-2050 (`c n e b a b p b a g e n e`) is still readable. x~2560 shows a minim before a 6-shape that has an ascender. I read that as `q b` (a q with a long descender, then b), not 16, because the first stroke descends well below the line.
- **L9**:
  - x~1050 `19`: a minim plus a 9 with an open bowl and a long tail.
  - `4o` at x~1360 (crossbar plus ring) is followed by `3o`.
  - x~1800 `{s/5}`: a short round s between b and n.
  - x~2080 `{b/d}`: a bowl with a faint thin upright (8x). By the section-1 rule this leans to b-with-ascender or d.
- **L10**:
  - Starts `64`.
  - x~1560 `19`: a minim with a dot (9x) before the 9 loop.
  - x~1700 `{b/y}`: a heavy blotted sign with an ascender and a bowl, next to the p.
  - x~2420 `{f/t}`: a long sloping stem with a crossbar running into the g loop.
  - The line ends `h 27 n`.
- **L11**:
  - Starts `{e/c}` at x~650: a c with a possible inner hook.
  - `25` occurs twice (x~940 and ~1280); the 5 stands next to the 2, so both are digit groups.
  - `3o` at x~1430. There is a separate small dot below the baseline at x~1460.
  - x~2420 `e`: a looped e with a long descender stroke from the line below crossing it.
- **L12**: `19` at x~880 and ~2240. `3o` at x~1610. The line ends `2o` at x~2700, with the ring at x-height.
- **L13**:

  - `17` at x~1780.
