- Doubt clusters in a few recurring families:
  - `{z/g}`: a 9-bowl whose tail sweeps down-left and then turns up in an open hook, not a closed loop. About 13 cases.
  - `{s/5}` / `{5/s}`: a round s with no digit next to it, and the pairs `{5/s} {9/z}` / `{5/s} {9/g}` (L4, L11, L18), which may each be the group 59.

  - `{y/d}` / `{d/y}`: a bowl with a joined ascender that is upright or leans forward.
  - `{n/a}`: pointed half-open forms.
- My own estimate is that 60-80 signs on the page are genuinely open.
