# B174r edition notes

Read from `images/9a179607_01.jpg` (rotated -0.25, own crops: every line at 2.2x for the running read, then every departure from C at 4x or more, 5x to 7x at the hard spots), against passes A, B and the frozen C. x = page pixels.

On the `!` marks: EDIT.md counts a sign outside the look-alike classes as an emendation, so `!` here covers two different things. They are listed separately in section 2: (a) readings where I think the sign I write is what stands on the page, but the change from C falls outside the classes; (b) places where the page does not show the sign the word needs.

## 1. Not read, or left open

- **L7 x~1830-1860, `{p/f}`**: a heavy blotted sign with a descender between `q n` (da) and `p u x` (chi). It reads like a false start of the `p` that follows, smudged out. Left as C has it; the text is "da chi me gli da" either way.
- **L14 x~1300-1340, `c {x/?} r`**: after "scriuer" stand a plain c, faint crossing hairlines with a heavy dot above them, and an r, then "cose". As written it decodes "p{i}e cose". I cannot tell what the word is (più? the sense is "refrained many times from writing [such] things"). Left open.
- **L18 `s e n k r f r` = "frazese"**: the word is franzese, but I see no `a` between `n` and the K-sign. Left as written (the scribe dropped the n, or it is lost in the join).
- **L19 `x a f g x n g b` = "incominstiato"**: the word is incominciato (or incomintiato). Between `a` and `x` there is a long sloping stem and a g with a closed lower loop, joined at the top. I cannot make the stem and the g into one `p`, so the signs stay as all three passes have them and the oddity stays in the text.
- **L20/L21 "se cr-/de"**: the sense is "come se crede uniuersalmente". L20 ends `f r p e` and L21 begins `q r`; no sign for the second e of "crede" is on the page at either line end (the L20 final is the e with a rising loop, followed only by its exit flourish). Not supplied.
- **L22 x~530, `?`** and **L24 x~1110, `?`**: blots inside "disfa?rla" and "particol?ar". Both words are complete without them, so the blots are probably cancelled signs.
- **L9 x~2160-2260, "partc{a/h}o[l]ari"**: the word is particolari. All three passes read `g p {n/u}` and then a bowl sign. As I see it, between the g and the long stem stands a short thin diagonal crossed by a faint second stroke with a dot above (the missing x), and what the passes call a smeared `{n/u}` is the blurred bowl of the p, so the signs would be `g x p b`. No pass has that x and all three have the smear as a sign, so I have not added the one or dropped the other; the doubt stays in the text.
- **L19 x~1620-1700, "do[l]esi"**: the word is dolersi. After the r there is a small hook before the f that could be an e, or only the r's flag. No pass has a sign there, so none is added.
- **L2 "andemen-ti"**: the sign after `q` is an r (one minim with a flag), not n. The scribe wrote andementi for andamenti. Kept.

### What the checker counts

Besides the classes and the emendations, the checker reports a few `regrouped`, `sign dropped` and `other`. None is a separate change: they are how its line diff aligns adjacent edits (for instance `b {1/i} h` to `61! f r` in L15, `h n` to `f r a`, `y b` to `p! b`). Every change outside the classes is one of the `!` rows above.

## 3. Code groups without a value

- **`21` (L7 x~470-520)**: a 2, a minim, then `g n`. The line break is "ha- / 21 ta", i.e. "hauuta opinione". The doubling groups in the key run 8, 5, 1o, 11, 12, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19, 2o (tt), 22 (zz), so `21` fits the series as **uu** (after tt and before zz). One instance on this page; worth checking on the other pages.

No other unvalued group remains in the text (the `2` of L18 is emended, see above).

## 4. Persons, places, events

- **V. Ex.**: Ferrante Gonzaga, the addressee. **S. M.ta** and **[Imperatore]** (L9): Charles V. **[Papa]** (L10): Paul III. **[Franza]**: Francis I's France.
- **"li soi ministri"** (L5): the emperor's ministers in Italy, to whom the cardinal has been passing intelligence on the pope's dealings with France.
- **"la dieta"** (L20): the imperial diet of 1545 (Worms), where the pope complains the emperor acts as head and stands united with the Lutherans.
- **"le promesse fatte in Busseto"** (L24-25): the meeting of Paul III and Charles V at Busseto, June 1543.
- The page runs on into B174v L1: "con-/tra li soi fedeli si uale delli soi maggiori nemici che son luterani".

## 5. Choices between two real words or forms

- **L5 "alli quali"**: C has `y h n d x` (by its heavy/thin ascender rule), which gives "luaqi". I read `d h n y x`. The first sign has a heavy sloping ascender, the second an a-bowl with a thin dotted upright; on this page the thin-upright form is certainly l in "gli" (L7 x~2190), so the rule does not hold here and the word decides.
- **L6 "habbino" / "habbia o"**: C `8 x n b` gives "habbia o ha-"; I read the sign as the pointed a (plain n): "habbino hauuta opinione".
- **L8 "o forsi"**: `b s b c f x` in C ("o fopsi"); the c is taken as e. "forse" is the alternative only if the last sign were r, and it is a clear x.
- **L12/L13 "son stato" and "sono restato"**: a/n in both (`f b a`). In L13 the null 24 stands inside "son[]o".
- **L15 "se m appresenta"**: C `h g n ... h n g n` ("utappreuata"). I read long-s + r, then a 9-bowl with an open left-swept tail (z, not g), then `n 17 e r`, long-s + r, a, g, n. "se t appresenta" (g) is the other reading; the tail has no closed loop.
- **L18 "per mostrar"**: C `g b f g c n e` ("tostpar"); first sign z (open tail), c as e. Then "se si scoprira" (C `h r f x`, "uesi"): the stem descends below the line and carries an r-shoulder, so `f r`.
- **L24 "li promesse"**: the article is `y x` (li), clear; kept.
- **L25 "guardi a non"**: could also be divided "guardia non"; the sense wants "guardi a non prouocar l ira di dio".
