# Sibling cc. 102-105 (record 2c1077f1): notes

Ercole Gonzaga to Ferrante, Mantua, 4 July 1544.

## What is in the record

| image | what |
|---|---|
| `_01` (c. 102r) | covering letter in clear, dated "Di Mantova il iiij di Luglio del xliiij"; it says the cipher is enclosed ("coll'inclusa zifra") |
| `_02` | its verso, blank (show-through) |
| `_03` (c. 103r) | the cipher, 26 lines, a cross at the head |
| `_04` | its verso, blank (show-through) |
| `_05` (c. 104r) | period decipherment, another hand, headed "Di mant.a co' l.re di iiij. di luglio", 24 lines |
| `_06` | its verso, a docket only |
| `_07`, `_08` | blank leaf with seal; address and docket |

There is one cipher and one decipherment, and they belong together: line 1 decodes by the swap to "io mi despero di [39] impedimento che da [L9] a [35] colle poche prouis-", which is the first line of the clear.

**The cipher does not continue beyond the clear.** Both end at "me ne sforza"; the cipher's last sign is the null 24. Every sign on the page is covered by the decipherment.

## Fit of the swap

All 26 lines decode by the swap of BRIEF.md. The decode of `sib102_transcription.txt` reads as continuous Italian and follows the clear word for word apart from the differences listed below. No letter needs a value outside the swap.

## New signs

- **`k`: a K-shaped letter sign for plain z.** An upright with a `<` attached to its right side, x-height plus a short ascender. Three times: L14 `c e b h r a k n` "Prouenza", L17 `n h n a k x` "auanzi", L26 `f s b e k n` "sforza". This is the place of cipher m in the swap (m-z); the scribe never writes an m-shape. Doubled z is the code 22 (L25 "discorsazzi").
- **`#` (L14, x~1960 on the deskewed page):** two tall parallel strokes with two short bars through the foot, standing where the clear has the "qu" of "quella": `68 s h # r 14 n` = "como fu qu-e-ll-a". Either a sign for "qu" or a cancelled `d h` written over; elsewhere qu is always `d h` (L11 qui, L16 quattrino, L25 questi). One occurrence, so undecided.
- **Doubling codes.** Besides 14 ll, 16 nn, 18 rr, 19 ss, 2o tt of glyphs.md, the page has **8 bb** (4 times, certain), **5 cc** (5 times, certain), **1o dd**, **11 ff** (twice), **12 gg**, **22 zz**. The run is alphabetical: 8 bb, [9 cc expected, but the sign is a flat-topped 5 every time], 10 dd, 11 ff, 12 gg, 14 ll, 16 nn, 18 rr, 19 ss, 20 tt, 22 zz. By that pattern 15 = mm and 17 = pp are predictions (13 and 21 unknown); none of them occurs here.
- **5 vs the letter s (plain f).** The doubling 5 has a long flat top stroke; the letter s is a small round s. L12 `s n 5 x n` "faccia" has both side by side.
- **L9 (Imperatore).** L1 x~1840 and L5 x~960. An L with a single small raised figure: a round bowl with a tail running down to the L's foot (clearest in L5), in L1 with a short tick on its left. It is not the `L10` of L18 (Papa), where a separate 1 and o are plain. If it is in fact a cramped "1o", then the Imperatore and Papa groups differ only in how the superscript is written, which would be a trap on the 1545 pages: any `L10` there should be looked at for the tail.
- **66 (L22).** Two 6-shapes where the clear has "può". Could be `6 b`; see the code table.
- **L8 last group**, where the clear has "io": a 3 followed by two joined minims (n-like), read `{3n/31}`.

## Signs that are not what the word needs (transcribed by shape)

- L2 `s {a/n}` "fa": second sign has a closed bowl.
- L4 `g r 2o r` for "terre": the doubling sign is plainly 2o (tt), giving "tette". Encipherer's slip for 18.
- L8 `z b y g {o/b}` "molto": last sign is a small ring without ascender.
- L9 `f {r/x}` and L5 `f {x/r}`: "si" / "se" cannot be told apart by shape here.
- L13 `y u {x/n} h r e r f g r` "l'hauereste": corrected, with a small insertion above the line (an x-like mark and a faint tall stroke).
- L17 `q 25 x`: the null 25 stands inside "di" (q, 25, x), unless a second x is hidden under the q.
- L17/18 "Carignano" runs over the line: `p n e x t {a/n}` | `n ? a b`; the second sign of L18 is under a blot (a deleted letter).
- L19 `g r e z x a b` "termino" where the clear has "termine".
- L20 `c e b z g x 19 x z n`: "promtissima" (z, the 9-shape), clear "prontiss.a".
- L22 `t e n g x {a/n}` "gratia" and `n 5 x {a/b}` "accio": both final signs look like a.
- L25: `x b` of "desiderio" is written above the line.

## Against glyphs.md

No contradiction. Confirmed: 14, 16, 18, 19, 2o, 3o, 4o, 53, 64, the nulls 24 and 27, bare L = et, L10 = Papa, L14 = Venetiani. The open items are settled as: 8 = bb (single digit), 39 = questo, 73 = anchor. With those, c. 96 L12 "39 ho 73 inteso" reads "questo ho anchor inteso" and L15 "de 8 a durare" reads "debba durare".

The `L43` / `L 43` question is not tested here: no full-size L followed by full-size digits occurs, and neither 43 nor 45 appears. The "Marchese" (del Vasto) and "Bransuich" (Brunswick) are spelled out in letters, and so are Carignano and Prouenza.
