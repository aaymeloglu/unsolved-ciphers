# A78r edition notes

Started from `transcription/A78r.C.txt`. Every departure was looked at on the prepared page
(the halves strips, plus 2.5-3.5x crops where needed). `check_edition.py` result: 0 mismatches,
63 departures (4.9%). 3 of them are emendations.

## 1. Unread or open words

- **L04 `il <L22> oiropi{u/g}ssonte`**: not read. The signs are clear (`b x e b c x {h/t} 19 b a g r`),
  but no look-alike swap I tried gives Italian. Sense: "...with which we had to defend [L22] ...,
  and it was seen that this Casale was in very great danger".
- **L03 `co a li quali`**: as written. Probably "con li quali" (the fanti), but no sign supports the n.
- **L10 `che genti sariano state quelle`**: C has `h a g x` ("unti"). The first sign is a tall stem
  with a bar, followed by a separate r: `t r a g x`, "genti" (compare L09 "genti imperiali", and
  the feminine "state quelle"). The same word in the same shapes opens A78v L26. Counted as
  regrouped.
- **L12 `il <L3o> gtrouasse`**: a cross-shaped sign (`t`) stands between L3o and "trouasse". It may be
  a stray or cancelled sign. Left in.
- **L16-17 `che uto obglieati ? [S. M.ta]`**: not read. Something like "what (I) owe / am obliged to
  H.M." `?` at the start of L17 is a blot.
- **L17 `le cose di ouatia in termino`**: "cose di" is certain. The next five signs `b h n g x`
  (+ `n`) are not read. On the image the second of them may be a cross-form t followed by an
  accented e (`b t e n g x` = "ograti"), which is still not a word. Left as C.
- **L19 `messer camillo lao{1/i} puo star`**: `y n b {1/i}` is not read. Expected something like
  "ella" or "laonde". The minim is left open.
- **L21 `uo per se possibil fusse`**: as written. The image has "f r" before "possibil", so it is
  "se". "uo per" (`h b c r e`) may be a slip for "uorrei". On the image the c may be an e, but
  "uorer" is no better, so the c is kept.
- **L27 `[che] nhsuuer?{e/l}i`**: probably "che n'hauerei". `f h` and the blot do not fit, so left
  as C.
- **L24 `persicolo`**: "pericolo" with a stray f (all three readers have a sign there). Left as written.

## 2. Emendations

- **L03 `e!` (`[r]e riseruati`)**: L02 ends "haue" and L03 begins `c r e x ...`. "hauere riseruati
  piu di duamilla fanti" needs e. At 3.5x the first sign is a plain c with a curled top and no eye.
- **L09 `t!` (`[g]enti imperiali`)**: the sign is a clear h (stem plus arch-leg) followed by r. The
  sense needs t (plain g). h/t is not a listed look-alike, although the passes do write `{h/t}`
  doubts elsewhere on this page (L04, L05).
- **L25 `s!` (`amico di [f]ortuna`)**: the page has c (plain p), giving "portuna". "parer poco
  seruitor di S. M.ta et amico di fortuna" needs s (plain f).

Departures marked "other" by the script:
- L08: `{l/d}` > e ("ricorrere per"). B had e as its alternative.
- L18: n > h ("udera", sic for "uedera"). B reads h, and the image shows a tall ascender.
- L29: u > 11 ("difficulta"). Two separate minims at 2.5x, as C's own note allowed.
- L09: 19 > 18 ("la terra"). The figure has a closed g-like lower loop, the 18 form of glyphs.md.

## 3. Unvalued code groups

- **`<28>`** (L01, inside "pensau<28>a" = pensaua): it sits inside a word whose letters are complete
  without it, so it is almost certainly a null, like 24, 25 and 27.
- **`<L3o>`** (L03, L08, L12, L17): a powerful person on the imperial side, close to Casale.
  - The contexts: "ricorrere per aiuto al L3o"; "sempre che il L3o trouasse forza a bastanza di
    ricorrer a lui per aiuto"; "il L3o ci puo aiutar et promette di farlo bisognando".
  - In 1545 that points to the imperial governor of Milan, Alfonso d'Avalos, marchese del Vasto:
    "il Marchese". This is an inference from context; nothing confirms it.
- **`<L22>`** (L04 "deffender il L22"; also B175v L11 "al L22"): a person or a territory to be
  defended. It is "al L22" in letter B, so probably a person. Not identified.
- **`67 58`** (L22): C wrote `6758` as one group. I split it as 67 "pur" + 58 "quando" ("pur quando
  s'hauesse a perder quello di Monferrato"). Both values are in key.tsv, and the sense fits. This
  is a regrouping only, with no change of sign.

## 4. Persons, places, events

- **Casale**: capital of Monferrato.
- **The Franzesi**: threatening to advance and "seguir la uittoria", which suggests a recent French
  success in Piedmont (Ceresole, April 1544).
- **il mastro di stalla**: the Gonzaga master of the horse, sent to Casale.
- **l'Abbadino**: the scribe wrote "abbadino". A Gonzaga agent who has reported to Ferrante.
- **il conte da Ladriano**: sic, for Landriano. The encipherer dropped the n.
- **Madama nostra la Marchesa**: Margherita Paleologa, marchesa of Monferrato, mother of the young
  duke.
- **messer Camillo**: an agent to whom copies are being sent. Not identified further.
- **il duca**: Francesco III Gonzaga, a minor under the cardinal's regency. In L28-29, "quando il
  duca sara in eta" is when he comes of age. The 27 there is a null, and the f n before it was
  read from A.

## 5. Choices between two real words

- **L13**: "labbadino" against "labbadiao". A read `a b`, and the image shows a closed bowl, so
  "l'Abbadino".
- **L05**: "pericolo se non pigliauamo". The page has `f n s 3`. I read the s as 5, joined to the 3
  as 53 "non", and the n as r ("se"). The alternative, "pericolosa f...", makes no sense.

## Summary

The French were expected to advance after their victory. The master of horse was sent to Casale
and fanti were held back to defend it. Casale was ordered to give the French victuals and passage
but to admit no imperial troops without the regent's consent, since help from L3o could not be
counted on. The cardinal now protests that he will do whatever H.M. requires. He would lay down
his life rather than fail his duty, and would rather not lose any of the family's states, even
Monferrato. He fears both looking like a poor servant of the Emperor who goes with fortune, and
the blame for losing a state while he governs for the minor duke.
