# B174r edition notes

Worked from `B174r.C.txt` with passes A and B beside it. Every line was read on 2x quarter-line
crops of the page, and the doubtful places again on 4x crops. `check_edition.py B174r` passes:
110 departures (9.3%), 3 of them emendations. 38 of the departures are d/y: on this page C wrote
`d` for the looped sign (plain l) from L5 on; the true d (plain q: quali L5, questa L5, questo L8,
quasi L10, querela L20) has a thick upright stem and no loop.

B174r is the first page of letter B. The last line ends "poi che con-" and runs on to B174v L1,
which begins `g c n y x` ("tpali"). With c read as e (c/e) that is "tra li": "poi che con|tra li
soi fedeli si uale delli soi maggiori nemici". B174v L1 should be changed to match.

## 1. Not read (open)

- **L9:** "troppo a partc{a/h}bn{x/s}a{q/r}i". The sense is "o pur che uenessi troppo a
  particolari" (or "particulari"). The place is washed out and has show-through; C has more signs
  between p and the final n than the word needs, and I could not settle them. Left as C has it.
- **L14:** "di scriuer p{i/?}ecou". Six signs `c ? r p b ?`; the second is a hairline with a dot
  and the last is a faded tall sign. The sense needs something like "son restato molte uolte di
  scriuer [...]", then "et sempre ho desiderato". Not read.
- **L2:** "intender{g/e} li". The sign between "intender" and "li" is a solid blot, probably a
  cancelled sign; if it stands it is r ("intendere li"). C's doubt is kept.
- **L7:** "da {c/s} chi me gli da". The sign before "chi" is a heavy blot shaped like p, most
  likely a p begun and cancelled before the scribe wrote `p u x`. C's doubt is kept.
- **L22:** "fa?rla" is "farla" (dis|farla): the `?` is a round blot over one sign, a deletion.
- **L24:** "particol?ar" is "particolar": again a large blot between y and `n e`.
- **L19:** "incominstiato" is "incominciato". Between `a` and `x n g b` stand a long doubled
  stroke and a looped sign (C: `f g`). The first may be a p retraced; I left C's two signs.

Written as it stands, though short of the word: L15 "appresata" (appresenta), L18 "frazese"
(franzese), L19 "dolesi" (dolersi; the same dropped r as "auetirme" on B174v), L20-21 "cr|de"
(crede, the e lost at the line break), L20 "nela", L21 "a lei stia farla" (stia a farla).

## 2. Emendations

- **L14 `t!`** "mi uen[g]a": the sign between `a` and the final `n` is a black blot (an overwritten
  sign; C read d). "ho desiderato che mi uenga occasione" needs t.
- **L15 `b!`** "[o]ccasione": the first sign of the line is a clear p (stem below the line), which
  gives "cccasione". The scribe's slip for b.
- **L15 `p!`** "[c]ol mezzo di V. Ex.": the sign is a faint tall stroke that rises above the line
  like a long f (C and A: f), which gives "sol mezzo". The phrase needs p.

## 3. Unvalued code groups

- **21 (L7): "uu".** L6 ends "habbino ha" and L7 goes on `21 g n`: "ha|21|ta opinione" is
  "hauuta opinione". 21 also falls in its place in the run of doubling groups, which follows the
  alphabet: 18 rr, 19 ss, 2o tt, [21 uu], 22 zz. Left as `<21>` because it is not in `key.tsv`.
- No other unvalued group. `61` (L15, "la quale se m appresata hora") is my reading of C's
  `b {1/i}`: the b and the figure 6 have one shape in this hand (compare 68 in L1) and a minim
  follows. The alternative `b x`/`b 1` gives nothing. 55 (L7) is kept against A's 53: the two
  figures are alike, and "et ben m ho sapputo imaginar la ragion" reads better than "non".
  L5: C and B have 74 (Italia) inside "ministri"; the figure is 24, the null, as A has it.

## 4. Persons, places, events

- **V. Ex.** is Ferrante Gonzaga; the writer is Cardinal Ercole. **S. M.ta** and **lo
  Imperatore** are Charles V, **il Papa** Paul III, **Franza** Francis I.
- L1-16: the cardinal has been sending Ferrante "auisi" on the Pope's dealings with France, got
  at his own cost and at risk to his informant. He thinks the Emperor's ministers in Italy have
  set little store by them and believe he is being duped; he has wondered himself whether his
  informant is deceived by those who report to him, and now sees a way to find out through Ferrante.
- L16-25: the informant reports a passage between Pope and Emperor. The Pope, to show that if he
  declares himself French he does so with good excuse, has begun to complain of the Emperor and
  has written to him some days since: that in the **dieta** (the Diet of Worms, 1545) the Emperor
  is held to be the head, with power to make and unmake it; that he sees it united with the
  **luterani** to the ruin of the Church; that all this is for private interest; and that the
  promises made at **Busseto** (the meeting of Paul III and Charles V, June 1543) are not kept.

## 5. Choices between two real readings

- **L2 and L6, "diligentia".** C has `25 x h n g x n` ("iuatia"), A `h r n`. The sign is a tall
  stem with a bar through it, then an r, then a/n. I took the barred stem as a tall t (`t r a`),
  which gives "gran dil(25)igentia" and "questa mia dil|igentia", the same shapes in both places.
  If it is h the word is "diliuentia", which is nothing. This is the largest judgement on the page.
- **L2 "ho usata":** C `u b h h g`, A `u b h f n g`. The second tall sign is a long f joined to n.
  "ho uuta" (hauuta) is the other candidate and the image is against it.
- **L15 "se m appresata":** `f r z n`; the third sign has the open tail of z, the g of "appresata"
  two signs on has a closed loop. With g it would be "se t appresata".
- **L18 "per mostrar se si scoprira":** C `c r e g b f g c n e h r` ("per tost par ue"). The fourth
  sign is z-shaped, c/e gives "strar", and C's h is a long f with r ("se").
- **L17 "d uno passato":** `q h a b`, a for C's n; "prima d'uno [negotio] passato tra 'l Papa et
  Imperatore", picking up "certo negotio" in L16.
- **L23 "senza rispetto":** C `h h a k n c x f r r`. The two h are long f + n and long f + r
  ("chie-sa se-nza"); the sign after the f of "rispetto" is a c crossed by the f's bar, not the
  v-shaped r that follows it. "dispetto" is not possible (no q).
- **Departures outside the listed classes** (class "other"), each on a clear shape: L3 final b
  (manifesto; A), L8 looped y for C's a (la ragion; A), L8 and L9 p for h (dicessi, piacesse; A),
  L9 19 for y (uenessi; B), L12 p for y (colui), L18 `p b a` for `y b 2` (con; B has p b and a
  doubtful third sign, which is faint), L19 y for a (dolesi; A), L20 d for a (querela).
