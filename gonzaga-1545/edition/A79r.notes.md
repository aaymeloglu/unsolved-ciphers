# A79r edition notes

x positions are in the processed (rotated -1.75 deg) page, as in `layout/A79r.layout.json` and
the C notes. Every departure was looked at in a 3-5x cut (scratch, not committed).

## 1. Not read, or read only as written

- L1 *uno de Pisa et l'altro da Liuorno*. The stem after `c x` in *Pisa* descends below the baseline into a rising stroke crossing the head of a separate n, giving f n (*sa*). In *da Liuorno* the sign before x is a joined loop with an upstroke and a rightward return, the same variant as the l of *il* in A78r 4. The rest reads `x h b e a b`; the h here has the short hooked foot as distinct from the f n of Pisa. A separate reading of the two crops also gives *Pisa* and *Liuorno*.
- L6–7 *i[n] la et ritornara qua*. After the x at the end of 6, the up-and-down arch is the n seen by all three readers. The required a (plain n) is therefore supplied. At 7 the first sign of *qua* has a heavy body and high upstroke but lacks the extended rightward foot of the preceding line's y in *la*. Read its regular look-alike d. L7 ends on a ligature whose final stroke remains unclear (*et … gli auisi*).
- L10 "parmi compredader": `p b z c e r q n q r e` is what is there; the first q (x~1940) is
  blotted but has a q's descender. "comprendere" is plainly meant but would need `a` for `q n`
  (two signs for one), so not emended.
- L11 *che si sa da persone*. The two successive groups have the same sloping long stem, crossing x in *si* and n in *sa*. The next sign has a separate descender and the 27 between its q and n in *da* is a null. An indefinite subject accounts for the reports from the count's intimates.
- L14 `ue dato` can be divided *u'è dato* (“an account has been given there”).


## 2. Emendations

- L3 x~1460 `q!` (an[d]ati): the scribe wrote a looped g (same shape as the following g at
  x~1610); "andati" needs q.
- L3 x~2290 `q!` (mo[d]o): pale but clear p (descender at left, bowl to the right); "per modo
  alcuno non" needs q.
- L4 x~1230, ~1270 `e!` `e!` (coniettu[r]a[r]): both signs are clear looped g's; the scribe
  enciphered "coniettutat". "po conietturar che habbino a passar il Po" needs e (plain r) twice.
- L4 x~2630 `r!` (conduc[e]ndo): two minims joined by an arch reaching the baseline, an n not an
  r; "conducendo artegliaria" needs r.

Look-alike departures, all checked on the image: L2 r->n "stato" (x~1160, one leg plus arch into
the g, n permitted; pass A n), p->q "da" (x~1520, blotted, descender at the left of the blot; pass
A {q/p}), n->a "ogni" (x~2225, the pointed half-open form; pass B a); L7 a->n "auisar" (x~2285,
pointed with small light interior; pass A {a/n}) and long h -> `f n` (x~2440; pass A f n); L8
n->a "perdoni" (x~2630, pointed; pass A a); L9 long h -> `f r` "dir se non" (x~1170) and n->a
"facendo" (x~1990, pointed; pass A a); L11 c->e "uarieta" (x~675, top curls into a small eye);
L12 long h -> `f r` "cose" (x~2060, faint bar on the stem) and "secrett-" (x~2490); L13 n->u
"pochi" (x~1410, open top, joined at foot: a clear u); L7 `{s/5} {z/9}` regrouped as 59 [quanto]
("di quanto intendero").

## 3. Code groups

At the end of 14 the raised 30 has a distinct short underline following the y of *al*. The upright that normally carries the number in L3o is absent or merged into the preceding y. The edition keeps both L3o (marchese del Vasto) and 3o (*che*). The marquis is a good contextual fit beside the duke of Florence, and the 4 May parallel says he too was kept informed (Barb. lat. 5792, f. 50r).


## 4. Persons, places, events

The sentence runs on from A78v ("perche mentre e stato la ui sono uenuti doi sconosciuti...").
"il conte" (L12) is the Count of Mirandola (Galeotto Pico) of A78v; "il Po", bridge and artillery:
a feared crossing of the Po by French-party troops. "duca di Firenze" (L15) is Cosimo I de'
Medici. The page ends the cipher of letter A.
