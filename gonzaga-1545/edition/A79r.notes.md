# A79r edition notes

x positions are in the processed (rotated -1.75 deg) page, as in `layout/A79r.layout.json` and
the C notes. Every departure was looked at in a 3-5x cut (scratch, not committed).

## 1. Not read, or read only as written

- L1 "uno de piu [et] laltro da noiuorno": the signs are clear and agree in all three passes
  (`c x h` with the open-bodied h; `q n a b x h b e a b`), but "da noiuorno" is no place or name
  I can identify. "de piu" may stand for "de' Pii" / "de Pio" (the Pio of Carpi), which would need
  b for h (a b/h look-alike), but the h at x~1215 has the open, foot-curled body, not b's closed
  bowl, so I left it. Both words open.
- L6-7 "tornara presto iala [et] ritornara lua": `x n y n` is clear (the n after x at x~2400 is a
  plain two-minim n, cut at 4x), and `y h n` at L7 x~1000-1130 is y + stem with two legs. "presto
  in la" would need a for n; "lua" is no word. Left as written; word division "iala"/"lua" is not
  a reading.
- L10 "parmi compredader": `p b z c e r q n q r e` is what is there; the first q (x~1940) is
  blotted but has a q's descender. "comprendere" is plainly meant but would need `a` for `q n`
  (two signs for one), so not emended.
- L11 "si u{e/a}da": the C doubt `{r/n}` (x~1680) kept open. "si ueda" or "si uada" are both
  possible; neither is compelling ("si ha da" would need u for h).
- L14 "ue dato": the long-stem sign at x~1700 has stem plus arch and leg (h). "s'e dato" would
  need f alone; kept h, read "u'e (v'è) dato conto".

## 2. Emendations

- L3 x~1460 `q!` (an[d]ati): the scribe wrote a looped g (same shape as the following g at
  x~1610); "andati" needs q.
- L3 x~2290 `q!` (mo[d]o): pale but clear p (descender at left, bowl to the right); "per modo
  alcuno non" needs q.
- L4 x~1230, ~1270 `e!` `e!` (coniettu[r]a[r]): both signs are clear looped g's; the scribe
  enciphered "coniettutat". "po conietturar che habbino a passar il Po" needs e (plain r) twice.
- L4 x~2630 `r!` (conduc[e]ndo): two minims joined by an arch reaching the baseline, an n not an
  r; "conducendo artegliaria" needs r.

Look-alike departures, all checked on the image: L2 r->n "stato" (x~1160, one leg plus arch into
the g, n permitted; pass A n), p->q "da" (x~1520, blotted, descender at the left of the blot; pass
A {q/p}), n->a "ogni" (x~2225, the pointed half-open form; pass B a); L7 a->n "auisar" (x~2285,
pointed with small light interior; pass A {a/n}) and long h -> `f n` (x~2440; pass A f n); L8
n->a "perdoni" (x~2630, pointed; pass A a); L9 long h -> `f r` "dir se non" (x~1170) and n->a
"facendo" (x~1990, pointed; pass A a); L11 c->e "uarieta" (x~675, top curls into a small eye);
L12 long h -> `f r` "cose" (x~2060, faint bar on the stem) and "secrett-" (x~2490); L13 n->u
"pochi" (x~1410, open top, joined at foot: a clear u); L7 `{s/5} {z/9}` regrouped as 59 [quanto]
("di quanto intendero").

## 3. Code groups

No group without a value on this page. One point: L14 end "conto al [che]" (x~2570-2760) is
ungrammatical. The 3o is raised above the line and the y's foot runs on as a long underline
beneath it, which is how the L + superscript groups look. This may be the group `L3o`, unvalued,
which occurs four times on A78r ("il L3o ... al quale bisognava defendere", "ricorrere per aiuto
al L3o", "il L3o ... ci puo aiutar"): someone the cardinal reports to and looks to for help
alongside the Duke of Florence; most likely the imperial governor of Milan (in 1545 the Marchese
del Vasto). Not put into the text: the sign before the 3o has a y's bowl, and L3o has no value
in the key.

## 4. Persons, places, events

The sentence runs on from A78v ("perche mentre e stato la ui sono uenuti doi sconosciuti...").
"il conte" (L12) is the Count of Mirandola (Galeotto Pico) of A78v; "il Po", bridge and artillery:
a feared crossing of the Po by French-party troops. "duca di Firenze" (L15) is Cosimo I de'
Medici. The page ends the cipher of letter A.

## 5. Choices between two real words

- L11 "si ueda" / "si uada" (left open, see 1).
- L1 "piu" / "Pio" (left as written, see 1).

## Reading

doi sconosciuti, uno de piu et l'altro da noiuorno; et per quello che gli e stato detto, da quelle
parti ogni di ne sono venuti et ve ne sono andati; et dice che per modo alcuno non po
conietturar che habbino a passar il Po, conducendo artegliaria con loro, che bisognaria far il
ponte; et non vede che vi si pensi. E gli tornara presto [iala] et ritornara [lua], et di quanto
intendero non manch[e]ro avisar. Et vi gli avisi gli parono contrarii, mi perdoni, che non posso
dir se non quello che intendo, facendogli intender per che via lo so. Ben parmi compre[n]der da
questa varieta d'avisi che si v[?]da persone intrinsichissime del conte, che le cose passino
secrettissime et che pochi sappino la verita delli loro dissegni. Di tutto v'e dato conto al
[che / L3o?] et ancora al duca di Firenze.
