# B175v: editor's notes

The page is the end of letter B. It runs on from B175r (r26 "...de denari al-" probably joins "meno" in
line 1) and breaks off at the foot of the page with "per": the text goes on to a leaf we do not have.

## Editorial decisions

The parallel account in [Barb. lat. 5792, f. 7v](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0016) continues on [f. 8r](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0017). It supplies an independent check on the events and several persons. Its wording differs throughout.

| Line | Reading and evidence |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | The repeated stem in *de [g]enti* matches B175r 26 and the register’s distinction between money and troops. The g remains supplied. *Loi* is the sequence y b x; pass A has y h x. The signs between *con* and *uincitore* are unresolved. *È ita* divides the existing signs. The first sign of *[q]uali* has a descender (cipher q for d), so the expected q is supplied. |
| 4–5 | Two consecutive figures give 96 (*guerra*) as at B175r 24–25. In *mezza*, the 9-shaped z after x lacks the separate initial minim visible in 19 in *grosso* earlier in the line. The register describes a tentative promise with *quasi intentione*. *Le fa un bon piede* retains the vowel written before *fa*. |
| 6–7 | *Accenna* uses the established 5 for cc and 16 for nn; pass B offered 5. The register explicitly uses *accenna*. In *bisogno* the bowl can be b and the crossed form can be t (also offered by pass B). The short run after *di* remains difficult. The end of line 7 has an open c and two touching movements giving *per*. |
| 8–10 | *Fracesi* retains the missing nasal. *Baulma* is written out. The cross floats above the baseline, alongside a larger cross in the margin; pass A likewise excludes it from the text. The register has *Monsignor della Baulma*. For *uenuta* the middle a/q was disputed in pass A. The preceding open bowl can be e as well as c, giving *era uenuta*, exactly as in the register. The doubling series predicts 15 = mm, with two target occurrences in the word for commission. The extra r in *commirssione* stays in the edition. The first letter of *[t]utti* is supplied against a q-shaped sign. |
| 11–12 | The e/c bowls and crossed stem give *pregaua*. Pass A has f n 17, giving *sappeuo*, with the doubled p retained. F. 8r has the equivalent *m’ha pregato … ch’io sapevo l’animo suo*. In *onde scrissi* a low arch is visible between b and q. |
| 14–16 | The 4-shaped sign (also offered by B) and following round form give 4o, *quello*. The q of *se[q]uestro* is supplied. *La* after *hauesser* uses alternatives already in pass C. The two digits after L make 64, the independently attested *perche*. The register uses *accio che*. |
| 17–19 | *Pa* and *preuarla* are left as written. Pass A reads 3o. The second-model review could not resolve the ring, so the code remains in doubt. The long joined form allows f r (*se*) in *sentendo*. The final bowl in *parlar* is e. The paired uprights in *il qual* allow y then d. Pass B reads n a at the start of line 19, giving *è t- / anto*. F. 8r confirms *qual è tanto obligato*. |
| 21–26 | The middle stroke of *lei* has a shoulder. The ending of *illustrissi…* is unresolved. *Nopera* and *Miladola* keep the apparent scribal slips. In line 25 the first sign is 6-shaped, followed by a free minim and a 9-shaped z; 61 z gives *quale m’ha mandato*. F. 8r also introduces the count’s answer with *quale*. Before *corsi*, pass A sees an initial f and an extra sign; with the b-shaped bowl, the group reads f b a 25 b (*sono*, with a null). The register uses different wording at the break. |

## Name codes and continuation

L22 stands for the State of Milan, and L54 for Granvelle. F. 8r identifies both at the corresponding places and calls the marquis *Marchese del Vasto*. The Modena cipher breaks off after *per*. The register’s following pages describe the count’s claims arising from the late count Giovanni Francesco’s failure to observe the imperial award, French recruitment at Mirandola, and correspondence about peace. The register dates the letter at Mantua on 9 January 1544 and continues with a postscript. See the [source audit](../source-search.md).
