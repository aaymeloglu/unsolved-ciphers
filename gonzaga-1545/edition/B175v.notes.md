# B175v: editor's notes

The page is the end of letter B. It runs on from B175r (r26 "...de denari al-" probably joins "meno" in
line 1) and breaks off at the foot of the page with "per": the text goes on to a leaf we do not have.

x positions below are in processed-page pixels (layout rotate 0.25).

## 1. Words and passages left open

- **L1 "deuatiloi"** ("meno che deuatiloi prouedera"). Line 1 x~980 is a thin ascender over a closed round
  body, so it is b, not h. That blocks "lui" (y h x). I found no word for it. "deuanti lui" would need two
  further changes.
- **L1-2 "con re-|ypestar uincitore"**. "con restar uincitore" is plainly what is meant. The line 2 signs are
  a clear tall l, then c r f g n e. No look-alike change gives "restar", so I left the signs as they stand.
- **L2 "[V. Ex.] eita per parentado"**. "eita" is not read.
- **L3 "d{u/o}ali"**. "le quali cose ... ha subito scritte" fits the feminine plural. But the first sign is a
  clear q with a descender (plain d), and cipher d (plain q) has none. The q is left as it stands.
- **L4 "ssezza"** (19 r 22 n, "dandogli ssezza intentione"). Perhaps a shortened "sicurezza". Not read.
- **L6-7 "e lia fenna esser bisuuao | di cado eli"**. Not read. L6 x~1900 could be short s or 5 by shape.
- **L8 "fpacasi"** ("fatto da fpacasi al cardinal"). Not read. "da Francia" does not fit the signs.
- **L8 "de la g baulma"**. The g (cross-form t) is the sign C placed from above the line. It may be an
  insertion, or a reference mark like the large cross in the left margin of L8. The name reads "de la
  Baulma" without it.
- **L9 "e pa ueduta"**. Not read: perhaps "et ha hauuta", but that does not fit the signs.
- **L9 "co<15>irssione"** = commissione. Before the 19 there is a faint extra sign, the e. Pass A has it too,
  so I did not drop it.
- **L11 "{s/?}{a/u}ppeuo"**. Presumably "sapeuo" ("poi che sapeuo l'animo suo"). The doubtful first two signs
  are left as C has them.
- **L12 "partiale o descrissi"**. Probably "partiale, e ne scrissi" or similar. Not forced.
- **L14 "a [che] dom ha risposto"**. The q of "dom" looks like a 4 (B has {q/4}). Neither "d'om" nor "4o"
  (quello) gives good sense.
- **L15 "hauesser{l/u}{a/?} mia ma"** and **"[et] odlo possa far meglio"**. Both left as C has them.
- **L17 "partisse pa inghilterra"** (= per?). Not forced.
- **L17 "con preuarla"**. The sense wants "pregarla". The h is clear, and h/t is not a look-alike class.
- **L18 "<3> nuntendo parla p di [questo]"**. All three passes have a bare 3 with no ring; pass A reads 3o.
  "che non 'ntendo"? (h for b would give "non tendo".) Not forced.
- **L18-19 "il lual et | eato obligato"**. The first part is "il qual" (y for d, as B175r "liluali",
  "luesta"). I did not change it without enlarging the place. "eato" is not read.
- **L21 "ringratio lni"**. Not read.
- **L22 "lustrissi{p/ss}{c/e}a"** = illustrissime a. The ink is a c, then a 9-shaped sign, then an exit
  hairline. A reads it as 19 r, B as c p. Neither gives "me", so it is left open.
- **L22 "la bona nopera"**. There is one a-sign too many for "la bona opera". Left as written.
- **L24 "miladola"** = Mirandola. One sign short of the name: an r/l and an n are missing. Not emended,
  since that would mean adding a sign.
- **L25 "li casi soi o ss ha mandato"** = "esso ha mandato"? Not forced.
- **L26 "u{a/n}o corsi"**. The interest "che ui sono / uanno corsi". The {a/n} is left.

## 2. Emendations

- **L10 `g!`** "[t]utti": the word needs plain t. The sign is a q with a straight descender and no lower
  loop, which the g shape does not permit.
- **L14 `d!`** "se[q]uestro": the sign is a dark closed bowl whose upright rises above x-height, so it is
  between a and d. a/d is not a listed class, so the change is marked as an emendation. The h before it
  is a long stem that runs below the baseline, read as f (f/h). "il sequestro" also matches L8 and L9.

Other departures, each checked at 2.5-3.5x:
- **h -> f r** in "cose" (L3, L13), "sequestro" (L8), "marchese" and "sequestra-" (L9), and "seruitori"
  (L10). Each is a sloping long stem of the h second form.
- **y -> d** for the upright-ascender d in "sequestro" (L8) and "sequestra-" (L9).
- **y -> e** in "scritto" (L7). The c-bowl's exit rises up and to the right, the e-final form.
- **q -> z** in "m'ha" (L7). The tail sweeps down and to the left.
- **e -> c** in "po star" (L7).
- **f -> t** in "meglio" (L16): a clear cross-form t.
- **u -> 11** in "uffitio" (L19): two separate minims.
- **o -> b** in "seruitori" (L10).
- **u -> a** in "ringratiandolo" (L20, A's reading) and "signorie" (L21).
- **b -> h** in "uostre" (L21, A's reading).
- **{5/s}{9/z} -> 59** (L23): the two digits stand close together and give "quanto altro mi conosca".
- The remaining changes are c/e and a/n, as listed by check_edition.

## 3. Unvalued code groups

- **15** (L9, "co 15 i(r) ssione"). It plainly means **mm** (commissione): a 1 + figure doubling sign like
  11, 14, 16, 17, 18 and 19.
- **L22** (L11, "li frutti de cardinali seruitori del re che sono nel L22"). A territory where the marchese
  can sequester benefices. The best guesses are **lo Stato di Milano** or **Piemonte**. L24 (Milano) is
  already used for the city, so Stato di Milano / Lombardia is my first guess. Evidence is thin.
- **L54** (L19, "esso signor ... obligato a L54 et a V. Ex. per l'uffitio fatto con S. M.ta"). A person
  who, alongside Ferrante, did a good office with the Emperor. Possibly the cardinal himself ("a me") or
  a named patron. Not determined.
- **bare 3** (L18). Probably 3o (che) with its ring missing.
- **43** after h in L3 ("cose 43 ha subito scritte al re"). Read as S. S.ta. It is full-size here, without
  the preceding L of c. 96. The sense fits the Pope writing to the King of France.

## 4. Persons, places, events

- **S. S.ta** (Paul III) writes to the King of France (the "re", as on B175r), urging him to bring "la mo"
  into Italy. The word "mo" recurs on B175r ("dar conto della mo", "seguitata la mo"); its sense is
  uncertain, perhaps "the enterprise/army".
- **Lo Imperatore**: Charles V. The French king "si cauara la mascara come lo Imperatore".
- **Saluiati**: most likely Cardinal Giovanni Salviati, pro-French and holding benefices in Lombardy.
- **Cardinal de la Baulma**: de La Baume. Cardinal Pierre de La Baume of Besançon died in 1544. The
  sequestered fruits may be his or his heir's.
- **Il marchese**: Alfonso d'Avalos, marchese del Vasto, governor of Milan, who ordered the sequester of
  the revenues of cardinals serving the King of France.
- **Sauoia**: the territory where the King holds ground.
- **Inghilterra**: someone departing for England, fitting the Anglo-French war of 1545.
- **Monsignor** (L20): "al detto monsignor" refers back to B175r or to L54.
- **Conte della Mirandola**: Galeotto II Pico, the French client. His "danni" and "interessi" close the page.

## 5. Choices between two real words

- **L7 "si po star" vs "si ro star"**. I chose po (e -> c): the shape is an open bowl with no clear eye.
- **L24 "intender al conte"**. "al conte" needs c -> e and n -> a. The "ho fatto intender ... palco" reading
  is meaningless.
- **L23-24 "quanto al|tro mi conosca"**. "tro" needs c -> e, where the shape shows an eye. "mi conosca"
  needs n -> a.
