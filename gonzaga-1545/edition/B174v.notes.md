# B174v edition notes

Worked from `B174v.C.txt` with passes A and B beside it; spots checked on the line strips
(`halves/B174v/`) and on 1.6x crops of the page rotated 0.5 degrees. The edition also uses 4x crops and the parallel in Barb. lat. 5792, ff. 6r–7r.

The page continues B174r ("...poi che con") and runs on to B175r. The word
broken across the pages is "con|tra": L1 opens `g e n`, the second sign being the c/e shape read
as e, so "poi che contra li soi fedeli si uale delli soi maggiori nemici".

## 1. Not read (open)

- **L8–9.** After “con suo honor” the first sign has the tall, curved foot of h, followed by b and a-fading-into-y. Pass A already reads `h b`. The rest of the line is too faint to settle. The register has “con honor suo voleva operar tutti quei mezzi” (f. 6v). The final f in the cipher’s “operars” is distinct.
- **L13.** The numeral 95 with its round 9 and baseline 5 separates from `p b y e r` (“col re”). The register reads “non vuole pace col Re ma sì bene finirla in ogni modo” (f. 6v). The r of “finirla” is written above a caret, while the third letter comes from a q-shaped sign (plain d). In “ogni” the crossbar of t leads directly into a, then x.
- **L15–16.** After 53 the solid high body and long straight descending stroke are q (plain d) as in pass A, giving “non dico”. The following sentence asks neither for the Emperor’s secrets nor whether the news about the letters is true. Pass A already has `{h/f}` before null 24; the adjacent shoulder resolves as `f r`. Pass B preserves the additional shoulder after d h in “queste”. The register shortens the sentence to “non intendo che mi scopra li secreti di S. M.tà” (f. 6v).
- **L17.** At the end of the line a falling hook crosses the long f before n, the f x ligature (“sia”). After “poi che e” on L25, the sign has a high bowl and a descender without the crossbar of f; pass A also has p (“chiaro”). F. 7r reads “poi ch’è chiaro”.
- **L21–22.** The cipher gives “intertener la pratica”. The e at the end of the first syllables has the raised exit that can be read as d, and the faint terminal sequence r e y n is visible, with A already offering y in the penultimate position. Compare f. 6v, “s’ho da tratener la pratica, o pur abbandonarla”. For “dir se[g]uiro”, the tall terminal e of “dir” is recorded in pass B. Pass A separates the long `f r` following it. The g of “seguiro” falls under an overwrite. The register paraphrases this as “tornerò a proposito”.
- **L15.** The cipher gives “auetirme”, lacking the r of “avertirme”.

## 2. Emendations

- **L5 “esse[g]uire”.** The sign is tall, with a looped bowl. It reads y (plain l) rather than the required crossed t. F. 6v has “havere principiato per esseguir”.
- **L13 “fi[n]irla”.** The sign between the two crosses is q. F. 6v supplies the word.
- **L16 “ques[t]e [l]ettre”.** The obscured descender in the first word lacks a secure g-loop. The next word begins with a descending q (plain d), where y gives the l.
- **L22 “se[g]uiro”.** The ink obscures the central sign. The two crosses and the normal e-final before b fix the rest of the future ending.

- **L1 "maggio[r]i":** the sign is a clear looped y in mid-word. Since it is not final, the e/y
  class does not cover it.
- **L2 "ris[p]osto":** the sign has a clear descender, so it is cipher p, but the word needs
  cipher c.
- **L5 "principiat[o]":** the sign after the g (C's 18 regrouped to g, the minim-plus-figure
  class) is a closed a-shape with no ascender. "principiato" needs b.
- **L7 "e [b]isogno":** C's `?` is an ink blot. "che è bisogno che tra il Re et lui prima se
  definiscano le risse" needs cipher o. The 1 after it is t, as pass B also allows ({t/1}).
- **L12 "[u]o-":** C read 16, pass A "? b". The word "vuole" (non uo-/le) needs h b, and the
  minim does not show an h's loop.
- **L13 "mo[d]o":** the sign is blotted.
- **L13 "in[t]erporsi":** the sign has a straight descender and no lower loop, so it is not g.
- **L19 "uo-/l[e]ntieri":** the shape is h-like. "volentieri" needs cipher r.
- **L19 "peric[o]lo":** the shape is a/o (pass B {o/a}). The word needs b.
- **L20 "dis[g]ratia":** the shape is a plain r. "se per disgratia si scoprisse" needs cipher t.
- **L25 "dicendo[g]li":** C reads r (no crossbar), pass A t. "dicendogli" needs t.
- **L25 "uo[g]lia":** the page has "u o o l i a". The second o-sign should be t.
- **L26 "a tempo n[u]ouo":** C's extra small c should be h (u).

## 3. Code groups

- **63 (L7, L20): "però".** L7 reads "se lo volesse far di novo; 63 che è bisogno che tra il Re
  et lui prima se definiscano le risse", which gives "però che" (because). L20 reads "la
  fariano malissimo; 63 la prego", which gives "però la prego" (therefore). One value fits
  both places, and 63 sits in the 6x word-code decade (60 qualche, 61 quale, 64 perche, 66
  puo, 67 pur).
- **L6 (L2).** The register spells out “Lutherani et il Re d’Inghilterra” (f. 6r). The Pope wrote the letter to which the Emperor replies.
- **95 (L13).** “Pace”, directly aligned at f. 6v. The sign forms also read as z and s in isolation.
- **63.** The register has “perciò” in the first place and “sì che” at the second; these support the connective sense but leave the precise spelling open.

## 4. Persons and events

- **Who is speaking, and what L1-7 says.** The Emperor (Charles V, L9) is reported as saying
  he is Christian and will risk his realms and his own life for the faith of Christ. This is
  set against his use of "soi maggiori nemici che son luterani". The text names the Diet ("la dieta") and England ("Inghilterra").
- **L10-12.** The Emperor cares little for what His Holiness says about the Diet and England.
  He marvels that the Pope sends Farnese there. L11 has an e inserted above a caret, plain r, giving “Fernese” across the line break. The register spells it “Farnese” (f. 6v). His journey, the Emperor says, will be wasted.
- **L14-22, the writer's request.** He asks Ferrante (V. Ex.) to tell him whether he is being
  deceived ("gabbato") and whether to persevere "in questo uffitio". If it is no service to
  His Majesty (S. M.ta, the Emperor), he would gladly drop it, because of the cost and the
  danger to many people if it were discovered.
- **L23-26.** He reports that S. S.tà has sent copies of his own letters and the Emperor's to
  "il Re" (France), saying that since the Emperor's mind is clear, the King should get ready
  in time and send an army into Italy.

## 5. Choices between two real words

- **L6-7 "saria per la ue-|nir".** I took d→e to get "per l'avenir". Pass A has e; C has d.
- **L9 "far quanto gli uenna ben".** I read "s n e 59 t y x h r 16 n 55", where C has
  "s n c s z ... s s". The s/5 and z/9 shapes cannot be told apart, and 59 (quanto) and 55
  (ben) give "et far quanto gli venga ben". "uenna" is as written.
- **L11-12 "fernes-|e in".** Pass A's {a/n} gives "in" rather than "ia".
- **L17 "et sella crede".** I took d→e (e/d class), giving "et s'ella crede" (V. Ex. = ella).
