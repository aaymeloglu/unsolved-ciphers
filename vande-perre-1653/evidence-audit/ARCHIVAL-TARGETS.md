# Retrieval specification: Van de Perre–De Brune,1653

Prepared 19 September 2026. This is a research checklist, not an order or sent enquiry.
Please retain old page numbers and obtain the whole letter, verso, address, enclosures,
and any attached Dutch copy or English translation. Modern folio numbers are unverified.

## Bodleian, Oxford

**First priority:** MS. Rawl. A.7, old p.47, copy of Van de Perre to Bruyne,17 October 1653,
explicitly catalogued partly Dutch/partly cipher. Exact item:
https://archives.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/repositories/2/archival_objects/820123 .
Question: can the complete item and any decipherment/key attachment be supplied as images?
We need the digits and original punctuation, not a transcription alone.

## Zeeuws Archief, Middelburg

Current access number **2**, Staten van Zeeland en Gecommitteerde Raden.

1. **3188**, J. de Brune, incoming letters 1652–1658, one folder.
   https://hdl.handle.net/21.12113/7EE8C3AD46AB40FC882D10532E14648A
   Determine whether it contains Paulus van de Perre's letters dated 29 August,
   3/10/17 October and 14/21 November 1653, or a cipher/key enclosure sent around 29 August.
   The sender's surname may appear as Perre, dePerre, vandePerre; the recipient as
   Brune, Bruyne, Bruijne. Request an item list or complete folder images if available.
2. **3101.1**, England diplomatic correspondence 1652–1656, one bundle.
   https://hdl.handle.net/21.12113/22565F7402054D48A6003DDC0EFA75F3
   Scope explicitly includes copies forwarded after Van de Perre's death by the other
   three envoys. Determine whether the returned papers include his private De Brune
   correspondence, outgoing-letter copies, cipher tables, or deciphered texts.

These entries are verified in the modern inventory. Their exposed IIIF manifests have
no image canvases. Neither bundle has been inspected internally. The fact that other
incoming letters were scanned in 2025 does not establish digitization of these two bundles.
