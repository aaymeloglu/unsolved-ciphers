## 2. Digitization and manuscript images

**No online manuscript images are exposed for 3188 in the inspected catalogue.** The item offers “Reserveren,” but no scan viewer. Its public IIIF manifest returns HTTP 200 with a valid description and an empty `items` array:

https://proxy.archieven.nl/iiif/239/7EE8C3AD46AB40FC882D10532E14648A

An IIIF link alone therefore does not mean the item is digitized. Searching 3188 produced one archival-description match and two separate file-text matches; those two hits are not evidence of images attached to 3188.

**[blocked] Manuscript-image inspection:** the target manifest provides zero image canvases. I could not inspect the letters, identify individual correspondents, or establish whether a cipher/key is inside this folder. This does not prove that no internal or unpublished digital copy exists.

## 3. Van de Perre correspondence: confirmed related entry

**Inventory 2, inv.nr. 3101.1, 1652–1656, 1 pak** is the strongest explicit Van de Perre match. Its note reads:

> Hierbij een bundel afschriften, welke na het overlijden van Van de Perre in 1653 door de drie overlevende gezanten met een begeleidend schrijven aan de Gecommitteerde Raden is gezonden.

This describes a bundle of copies sent after Van de Perre's death in 1653 by the three surviving envoys, with a covering letter to the Gecommitteerde Raden.

- Permanent URL: https://hdl.handle.net/21.12113/22565F7402054D48A6003DDC0EFA75F3
- Exact catalogue URL: https://www.zeeuwsarchief.nl/onderzoek-het-zelf/archief/?mialg=&mivast=239&mizig=210&miadt=239&miview=inv2&milang=nl&micode=2&minr=1302310&miaet=1
- IIIF manifest, also with empty `items`: https://proxy.archieven.nl/iiif/239/22565F7402054D48A6003DDC0EFA75F3

Parent series **3099–3120** comprises letters from the ministers of State in England/Great Britain to the States, their Gecommitteerde Raden, or the raadpensionaris, with enclosures, **1591–1761**, 11 packets and 11 volumes. Its note says letters often duplicate those sent to the States-General or Holland; even letters headed “Copie” can be signed by the envoy and addressed to Zeeland.

Parent URL: https://www.zeeuwsarchief.nl/onderzoek-het-zelf/archief/?mizig=210&miadt=239&miaet=1&micode=2&minr=1302305&miview=inv2

The parent reports three digitized units and 1,431 files across the whole series, not specifically 3101.1. No connection to a particular surviving cipher letter is established by these descriptions.

## 4. Cipher-key search and related leads

**No explicitly described cipher-key entry was identified in inventory 2.** I checked the catalogue and downloaded the complete official EAD XML, searching variants including sleutel, cijfer, cyfer, chiffre, cipher, cypher, geheimschrift, clavis, alphabet/alfabet, and codeboek. Matches concerned ordinary keys, accounts, indexes, or confidential administrative records rather than an identified cryptographic key. This is a catalogue-level negative result, not a claim that keys cannot occur inside undescribed packets.

Two additional contextual leads, not confirmed cipher-key holdings:

- **3101.2, 1657–1663, 1 pak:** continuation of the English diplomatic correspondence. https://hdl.handle.net/21.12113/77ADE6A5D4AB4FB695559CB306CE4873
