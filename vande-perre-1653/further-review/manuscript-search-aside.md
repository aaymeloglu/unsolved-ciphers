# Van de Perre–De Bruyne, 1653: manuscript availability

Research date: 19 September 2026. Read-only investigation of Bodleian Archives & Manuscripts, Digital Bodleian, and digitized Macray. No messages, purchases, image orders, or requests submitted.

## Result and evidence boundary

**No publicly accessible digital images of the four requested manuscript locations were found.** Volume catalogue records and relevant digitized Macray catalogue pages were inspected. Neither target cipher was seen in manuscript. This is a discovery result, not proof that no unpublished, unindexed, or offline photography exists.

- **A.10 old p.471**, Van de Perre to Johan de Bruyne, 10 October 1653, Birch I, pp.522–523: supplied run `6.12.7.15.17.12.5.28.17.6.3.15.7.17.27` **not independently verified**. The A.10 identification has a chronological warning below.
- **A.8 old p.102**, same correspondents, 11/21 November 1653, Birch I, p.582: supplied run `21.27.7` **not independently verified**.
- **[blocked: target manuscript images not located]** No actual handwritten digits, grouping/punctuation, surrounding clear Dutch, or manuscript translation can be transcribed or translated from the evidence found. No printed wording is represented here as manuscript evidence. Target autograph/copy/translation status and modern foliation remain unverified.

## 3. Digital Bodleian searches

- Rawlinson collection: https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/collections/rawlinson/
- Global search: https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/search/

**Rawl. A.** returned **16 items** in both collection and global searches. All were inspected in the result lists: A.27, 203, 228, 289, 301, 359, 375, 384, 386, 387b, 389, 392, 402, 417, 431, 491. None was A.5, A.6, A.8, or A.10. Partial digitizations were included, not just fully digitized volumes. Global **Thurloe** returned two unrelated Godwin diary hits (Abinger e.21 and e.27).

Submitting these search terms did not change the URL; reproduce using the terms above. The site warned that technical issues could slow images or affect features. Results were coherent, but this warning further counsels against claiming definitive non-digitization.

## 4. Useful related entries, not substitutes

### Explicit cipher witness

**MS. Rawl. A.7, p.47**, copy of Van de Perre to Bruyne, **17 October 1653**:
https://archives.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/repositories/2/archival_objects/820123

The catalogue explicitly says **“Partly in Dutch and partly in cipher.”** Macray col.12, no.4 (scan n13 above) agrees. This is a different letter; no manuscript image or actual cipher characters were seen.

### Other nearby evidence

- **A.8 p.88**, extract from Van de Perre to pensionary Bruyne, **7 November 1653**, catalogued Dutch: https://archives.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/repositories/2/archival_objects/820134. Macray col.13, no.2: “Extract, in Dutch ...”. Not the 11/21 November target.
- **A.6 p.195**, undated extract from Van de Perre to Bruyne, pensionary of Zealand: https://archives.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/repositories/2/archival_objects/820091. Modern search result inspected; Macray col.11, no.10 explicitly describes a letter in Dutch.
- Macray col.11, no.24: **A.6 p.293**, translation of a note from de Bruyne to Van de Perre, **27 September**. Description only; translated text not seen.

### A.10 reference warning

Both Macray and the modern record assign A.10 chiefly to **December 1653–February 1654**. Macray col.12 meanwhile lists in **A.6**:

- no.36, **p.463**, translation of Van de Perre to the Admiralty of Zealand concerning exchange of prisoners, **10 October**. Modern search-result URL: https://archives.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/repositories/2/archival_objects/820117
- no.38, **p.474**, translation of Jongestall to Van de Perre, **10 October**. Modern search-result URL: https://archives.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/repositories/2/archival_objects/820119

The nearby dates/pages make **A.6 versus A.10 worth checking against Birch's actual source note and errata**. They do **not** prove the target old p.471 is in A.6; out-of-order material remains possible. No correction is asserted.
