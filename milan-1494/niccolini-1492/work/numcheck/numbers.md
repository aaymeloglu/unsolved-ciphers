How I read the pages: I looked only at the 9 images, zooming crops 2-7x with Pillow (some with autocontrast). Line numbers count main-text lines from the top. Interlinear gloss lines are not counted. x is the approximate pixel position on the 1272-px image. "s.L^ro" means: s, dot, capital L, superscript r-o (or just o). For each entry, the Gloss column gives what stands above the number, or immediately next to it, as written.

Scope: I list numbers that stand as separate tokens, set off by spaces, dots or colons from clear words or from a cipher run. Cipher runs separated by ÷ also contain digit strings (11, 114, 1145, 4906 and so on). Those are cipher glyphs, so I do not list them, except where a group is set off by spaces or dots and could be a number.

## Main pattern
