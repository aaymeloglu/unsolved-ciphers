# Bare runs (c.90r, 104r, 177r, 200r): blind transcription notes

Source: crops/bare only. Zoom script work/transcriber/z2.py, zooms in work/transcriber/out/. Same conventions and labels as notes-176r.md (N1-N8 keep their 176r meanings). These leaves are sharper than 176r, so dots are mostly clear.

Conventions carried over: ÷ with dots above and below = `S59{S12}?`; dash with dot above only = `S59`, or `S59{S65}?` when the dot sits over the right end; dash with dot below only = `S47{S67}?`; "11" written together = S60; 9 with a descender = S37{S10}?.

## New labels (skipping N11/N12 to avoid clashing with number labels)
- **N9**: 2/z-like sign with a small loop or hook at the top left, a zigzag body and a long diagonal tail out to the lower right (090r L15 x~1240-1300). Alternatives S66 / S30 ("2 with a long tail ..."), but no raised c or d is visible.
- **N10**: + cross whose stem is a heavy slanted stroke descending below the line, with a small raised c (or open o) above the right arm (090r L17 x~720-780). Faint tick below it. Alternative S27 + a separate raised c.
- **N13**: small closed o (or a) with a long horizontal tail running right at mid height into the next sign ("o—"). 090r L14 x~3150-3220 (looks more like a: alternative S76), 090r L17 x~2650-2700 and 2920-3000, 177r x~1680-1760, 200r x~1170-1250. The tail might just be a joining stroke; if so, read it as S81.
- **N15**: free-standing "15" between spaces (090r B9). Alternative S38 + S54 (the "5" is the same stem-with-flag-and-hook as S54).
- **N22**: free-standing "22" between spaces. Equivalent to inventory S70, given as alternative. 090r B3 (x~1110-1170, mid-run but spaced off on both sides), 090r B7 (x~820-880, run end), 177r B1 (x~290-340, line start).
