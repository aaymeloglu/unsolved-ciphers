# c.176r blind transcription notes (neutral labels)

Source: crops/masked only. Zooms made with work/transcriber/z.py (output in work/transcriber/out/). Images are soft (upscaled from a 1272 px page), so fine detail (dots, small ticks) is often marginal.

Conventions
- `Sxx{Syy}?` = best label, then alternative(s); `+` joins a multi-sign alternative (e.g. `S60{S38+S38}?` = one sign "11", or two separate ı).
- "÷" with a dot above and a dot below is recorded `S59{S12}?` (S59 allows a dot below; S12 is "dots not made out"). Dash with dot above only = `S59`. Dash with dot below only = `S47{S67}?`.
- "11" (two short strokes written together) is always recorded as S60, alternative two S38.
- Digit-like "9" with a straight descender running below the line: `S37{S10}?`.
- Tall slanted stem ending in a hook at the foot, usually with a bar/arm to the right partway up ("5"/"h"-like): S54 (alternatives S31/S72 noted where the arm looks more like an h-crossbar or a baseline arm).

## New labels
- **N1**: "#"/"ff" sign. Two to four tall slanted parallel strokes crossed by one horizontal bar; the first stroke often descends well below the line. Ends runs at L19 (B4), L22 (B8), L28 (B11); also L17 next to B1.
- **N2**: b with a long horizontal bar across the ascender (L20 B6 only; see doubt there, the bar may be the foot of a descender from L19).
- **N3**: small 4/t-like sign. Short stem, a horizontal arm to the right near the top, short leftward foot; does not descend far (L21 B7, x~750-800). Alternative S72 or a small S32.
- **N4**: horizontal dash carrying a small e-like loop or hump in its middle ("-e-"). L20 B6 (x~1455-1535, hump rather than closed loop), L21 B7 (x~1690-1760), L22 B8 (x~1375-1445, 2390-2475 with a small tick above, 2900-2970, 3230-3310).
- **N5**: short horizontal dash with a short downstroke at its left end (r/Γ-like). L21 B7 last sign (x~2330-2380).
- **N6**: x with a short bar above it (L22 B8, x~2000-2045). Alternative plain S62 with a stray stroke.
- **N7**: cluster of two to three dots (colon / triangle of dots), L23 B9 last sign (x~820-845). Could be punctuation.
- **N8**: tall slanted stem with a hook at its foot, carrying a small raised o at upper right. L17 (x~1000-1085), L19 B2 sign 2 (alt S24 y-like; there the stem rises well above the line with the o at its top), L21 B7 (x~2120-2190), L28 B11 (x~620-690). Could be S54 + a separate raised o.
