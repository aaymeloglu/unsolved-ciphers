## What changes in the bare reading

- c.90r word 20, "per" (S75{S43}): doc 97 gives this label **ll** three times and never "per". "per non sendo
  apto" therefore rests on the sheet value alone, and that value is contradicted in this hand. Regraded to doubt (M).
- c.90r word 8, "dal" (S34{S49} S45 S69): **d is not a listed value** of S34 or S49. It stays lenient. Doc 97 gives
  S34{S49} = d six times, so the reading has after-the-fact support.
- c.90r words 23 and 29, "apto" (6 + long-tailed 3): doc 97 gives the long-tailed 3 = e four times. Both stay
  lenient; the reading is weaker.
- "che" (S23), "et" (S26): no legible doc 97 case; unchanged.
- sendo (S55 = d), quanta (S76 = q), frate/parte/se (N13/S19 = e), havervi (S36 = h): confirmed after the fact.
