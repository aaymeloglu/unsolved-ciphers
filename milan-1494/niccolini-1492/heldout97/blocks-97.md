# Niccolini, MAP LXXIV doc. 97 - held-out cipher blocks (crop index)

Letter: Angelo Niccolini ("Angelus Niccolinus U. d. ex or[ator]", signature at the foot of c.222v)
to Piero de' Medici (address on c.232v: "Magnifico Piero de' Medici ... Florentie"). Dated on c.222v
"... die xx Julij 1494" (last digit has the same form as the second digit 4); endorsement on c.232v
"1494 / Agnolo Niccolini / ... a di 22 di luglio".

Cipher with gloss: c.222r (lines from y~345 to the foot of the page) and c.222v (first six lines).
c.232r and c.232v carry no cipher (address, endorsement, seal; 232r is otherwise blank). The lower part of
c.222v (clear text, dateline, signature) carries no cipher.

Crops: `crops/unmasked/` (gloss present) and `crops/masked/` (gloss painted out), one deskewed line band
each, 3x LANCZOS, 3390 px wide. Crop x = 3 * (page x - 120), approximately (the band is rotated about page
x=290, so the right end drifts a few px). The cipher body sits at crop y ~144 of 222.
Crop names: `222r_Cnn` = nn-th cipher line on c.222r counted from the top; `222r_L02` / `222r_L08` are clear
lines with a short cipher group (L02 = the clear line just above C01; L08 = the clear line between C07 and C08).

Boundary words are the clear words on either side (rough readings, "?" = doubtful). Where a run fills the
line, the neighbours are the cipher lines above and below. Sign readings are deliberately not given.

## How block extent was set (reading of the preregistered rule)
The gloss on this letter is a compact running decipherment written in the space above each cipher line, not
letter-over-sign: it is written smaller than the cipher, starts at the left margin and stops at 45-85% of the
line width, and a drawn filler line in gloss ink runs from its end to the margin. The gloss text runs on
from one line to the next with no gap (sense continues across line breaks), so each gloss line renders the
whole cipher line under it. I therefore take the block = the whole cipher run of the line, from the first to
the last cipher sign, not the x-extent of the gloss ink. No line has a cipher tail that the gloss sense leaves
out (I could not detect one), so no tails were cut. Both extents are given below.

## Excluded name numbers / word signs
None excluded. Because the gloss is not aligned sign-over-letter, a name or title can only be dropped if a
distinct sign form for it is identifiable in the cipher, and I found none I could pin down without reading
sign values. Words in the gloss that are names or titles and MIGHT be word signs in the cipher are flagged
per block ("possible word sign"), by gloss position; the reference keeps them spelled out.
