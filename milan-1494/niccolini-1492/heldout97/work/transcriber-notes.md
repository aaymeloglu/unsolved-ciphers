# Doc. 97 (c.222r / 222v) blind transcription notes

Source: heldout97/crops/masked only. Script: work/transcriber/stack.py (in the niccolini-1492 folder). Zooms are in heldout97/work/tr/, each block in 2 x 3 strips at 2x. Labels are kept from 176r and bare-A, and the same conventions apply:
- ÷ with dots above and below = `S59{S12}?`.
- Dash with a dot above only = S59.
- Dash with a dot below only = `S47{S67}?`. This hand writes it very often as an arched or hooded dash ("⌒ with a dot under it").
- "11" = S60.
- 9 with a descender = S37.
- Stem with a flag and a foot hook ("5/h"-like) = S54.

General confidence is medium. The hand is regular, but the 3x upscale is soft. The painted gloss patches sit right on the top of the band, so ascenders and dots above the dashes are often clipped or merged with the paint (diamond-shaped paint blobs are mask artefacts, not dots). I classed a dash with a mark above as S59 only when a separate dot or tick was visible below the paint line; otherwise S47/S67 or a plain dash class. Expect some S59/S47 confusion in every block.

## How existing labels are used in this hand
- **N4:** horizontal dash with a small loop or "a" sitting on it ("—a—"). Very frequent here, the same shape as on 176r.
- **N13:** small o with a long tail to the right ("o—"). Frequent.
- **N1:** "#"/ff sign, 2-3 slanted strokes with a crossbar. In B13 and B21 it starts the line. In B30 and B29 it is doubtful (N1?).
- **N8:** stem with a hook plus a raised small o. At line ends in B1 and B11, and in mid-line in B1, B13 and B27. blocks.md's "raised o" candidates are these.
- **N7:** isolated dot(s) between signs (B3, B6?, B21). It may be punctuation.
- **S36/S52 ("L/⊥" family):** a stem on a long base stroke. Recorded as S36 when the stem stands mid-base (⊥) and as S52 when the stem is at the left (L).
- **S79** (Λ with a dot inside) appears as an "A/∩ with a dot": B14 x~950, B21 line end, B15 (doubtful).
- **S23** (B) is common. S78 (b/3 with ring) is given as an alternative where the B has a top loop.
- **S11 (π):** the π here often has a long top bar that extends right over the next sign. I counted it as one π; a few may hide a following short sign.
- **S66** "2 with a long tail and a raised c": a 2/z body on a long dash with a raised c (B7 x~690, B12 x~2340). In B6 x~1990 and B11 x~3000 the same shape had first been split as dash + raised c; I now give `S66{N4+N16}?`.
