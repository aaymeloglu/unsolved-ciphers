# Search for Gabbrielli key no. 120 (Roberto Acciaioli, 1512-1514)

Searched 2026-11-20. Status: **located and transcribed** ([gabbrielli-120.md](gabbrielli-120.md)). DECODE record R5806 is
catalogued as `ASFi_SIVol1_120`, but its two images show key 119 (Francesco Guicciardini) and that key's verso; key 120
itself is the two images of the next record, R5807. The rest of this file is the search as it ran, before the images
were opened. Its full-size images need a DECODE login; only thumbnails are served without
one. This search did not log in and holds no transcription; the full images were fetched
separately with the repo's `tools/decode.py`. The key is not on Yale Dataverse.

## What the compilation is

- Pietro Domenico Gabbrielli (abate, Florentine archivist), *Alfabeti che servono a spiegare le
  lettere in cifra del carteggio dei Dieci di Balia dal 1424 al 1530*, vol. I. Dated 1862 in the
  DECODE record note ("fatti dall'Abb. Pietro Domenico Gabbrielli, l'anno 1862") and 1864 in the
  Ilardi guide. Held by the Archivio di Stato di Firenze.
- Vol. II is a different set: "Alfabeti ... del carteggio degl'Otto di Pratica dal 1482 al 1530 e
  della Signoria, Missive, dal 1481 al 1530 e Responsive dal 1414 al 1530 (1864)" = Ilardi reel 93.
  No. 120 is **not** in vol. II. All Dieci di Balia keys, nos. 1 to about 194, are in vol. I.
- Ilardi Microfilm Collection (Yale, Sterling Memorial Library microform room): reel 58 = vol. I,
  under the heading "DIECI DI BALIA, CARTEGGI". The microfilm target reads "Archivio di Stato di
  Firenze / Chiavi delle cifre delle lettere ai Dieci di Balia dal 1424 al 1530 / Volume n. 1".
- DECODE names the volume `ASFi_SIVol1`. Other ASFi sets there are `ASFi_SIVol6` ("Chiavi delle
  cifre del carteggio dei segretari ...") and `ASFi_SIIVol2`, `ASFi_SIIVol9` ("Governo Mediceo -
  cifre de' secoli XVII-XVIII ..."). My reading, not confirmed by an inventory: SI/SII = serie I /
  serie II of an ASFi cipher-key collection, vol. 1 of serie I being Gabbrielli's vol. I. I found
  no ASFi inventory that gives the shelfmark (see below), so the exact modern segnatura is open.

## What the index says

Index images on Yale Dataverse (`58-index1.pdf`, `58-index2.pdf`, `58-Index3.pdf`; datafile ids
8002, 7983, 7988). Title page: "Indice degli scrittori in cifra del carteggio dei Dieci di Balia".
It is bound in the same volume, after the keys. Columns:

| Numero d'ordine | Nome | Anno | Numero delle Filze | Osservazioni |
|---|---|---|---|---|

"Numero d'ordine" is the key number in the volume. "Numero delle Filze" are the filze of the
letters the key opens, with no folio or page. They match ASFi, Dieci di Balia, Responsive
(1424-1530, 151 pieces, the same span as the volume title): in inventory N/9 filza 101 is
1 July-31 Aug 1510, and a Machiavelli letter from France of 13 Aug 1510 is cited elsewhere as
"Dieci di Balia, Responsive, 101".

Roberto Acciaioli entries, read from index image 2 (letter A), rotated and enlarged:

| No. | Nome | Anno | Filze |
|---|---|---|---|
| 89 | Acciaioli Roberto | 1505. 1507. 1510. 1512. | 82. 87. 88. 89. 98. 99. 100. 101. 113. |
| 111 | Acciaioli Roberto | 1511. 1512. | 102. 103. 104. 105. 106. 107. 108. 109. 110. 111. 112. |
| **120** | **Acciaioli Roberto** | **1512. 1513. 1514.** | **107. 108. 110. 113. 114. 115. 116. 117.** |

Osservazioni is blank for all three. Dates of those filze in inventory N/9: 107 = Nov-Dec 1511;
108 = Jan-Feb 1512; 110 = May-June 1512; 113 = Oct-Dec 1512; 114 = Jan-Mar 1513 (plus 27-28 Mar
1514); 115 = Apr-May 1513; 116 = June-July 1513; 117 = Aug-Sep 1513. Filza 118 (Oct-Dec 1513
and 1514) is not in the entry, so Gabbrielli did not cite a letter of October 1513 for key 120.
Nos. 89 and 111 overlap 120 in filze 107, 108, 110, 113, so all three are worth having.

Other entries on the same index pages (for orientation): 77 Acciaioli Giovanni 1504; 13 and 25
Alamanni Pietro 1483, 1492; 186 and 189 Alamanni Luigi 1529-1530; 60 Albizzi Luca d'Antonio 1502;
133, 139, 140 Albizzi Ant. Francesco 1527; 190 Albizzi Piero di Banco 1529-1530; many "Anonimo".

## Where no. 120 is

| Copy | Reference | Access |
|---|---|---|
| DECODE | R5806, `ASFi_SIVol1_120`, dates 1512-1514, 2 pages, image id I31325 ([record](https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5806)) | Metadata and 200x266 thumbnails open; full images "Authentication required" |
| Yale microfilm | Ilardi reel 58, well past the frames scanned for Dataverse | On site, or scan request |
| Original | ASFi, Gabbrielli, *Alfabeti ...* vol. I, no. 120 | Reading room, or reproduction request |

DECODE numbering: `ASFi_SIVol1_N` = record R(5686+N). Checked for N = 1, 2, 47, 48, 89, 109, 111,
112, 114, 119-126, 190-192, 194. So no. 89 = R5775 (dated "1505 -", 1 page) and no. 111 = R5797
(dated "1509 -", 2 pages). R5807 and R5808 (nos. 121, 122) are also dated 1512-1514; the index
pages I have do not show whose they are.

Thumbnails of R5806 saved as `images/gabbrielli/decode_R5806_thumb_P1.jpg`, `_P2.jpg` and an
enlargement `decode_R5806_thumbs_x4.png`. They show a ruled page with a heading, a printed A-Z
alphabet strip with two or three rows of signs under it, a short block of running lines, and a
nomenclator in several columns (roughly 25 rows by 3 columns on page 1 and about
20 more entries on page 2). Nothing in them is legible, so no values are recorded.

## What was checked

Yale Dataverse (plain GET, no login):

- `api/datasets/:persistentId/?persistentId=doi:10.60600/YU/XKVOWE`: dataset 7966, "Reel 58:
  Archivio e stato di Firenze Chiavi delle Cifre delle lettere ai dieci di balia dal 1424 al 1530
  Volume 1", deposited by Claire Bowern 2024-01-02, 54 files: `58-1.pdf` to `58-51.pdf` and three
  index images. Depositor's note: "There are about 150 ciphers; I scanned the first 45, they are
  arranged in chronological order. There is also an index by person, which I copied the first
  part of".
- `58-1.pdf` (id 7976) is the microfilm target; `58-51.pdf` (id 8004) shows key no. 47, dated
  1496. So Dataverse stops at no. 47.
- `api/search?q=Ilardi`, `q=cifre OR cifra OR cipher OR Chiavi`, and `q=*&subtree=voynichverse`
  with `type=dataset`: the collection "voynichverse" has four datasets. Besides reel 58: Ilardi
  reel 207 (Carte Strozziane serie III no. 249, first 40 pages, doi:10.60600/YU/ELZUUT), reel 1503
  (Siena, Lettere cifrate, doi:10.60600/YU/QDOHXM), and "Topic modeling in the Voynich
  manuscript" (doi:10.60600/YU/PXULND). None holds no. 120.
- `api/dataverses/voynichverse/contents` returned HTTP 202 with an empty body (failed).

Ilardi guide: `www.library.yale.edu/Ilardi/il-text.html` is gone from Yale (404); read from the
Wayback Machine capture of 2020-04-14. It gives the reel 58 and reel 93 descriptions quoted
above. The Yale catalogue record (hdl.handle.net/10079/bibid/3243613) returned 403 to curl.

DECODE (no login): `decrypt-web/RecordsView/<id>` pages for the records listed above;
`decrypt-custom/filesrv/?file=TH_IMG_R5806_I31325_P1.jpg` and `_P2.jpg` (thumbnails, HTTP 200).
I did not request the full-size files.

Cryptiana, `cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/italian.htm`: lists the DECODE ASFi key records
(R5687-R6316, R6318-R7018, R7020, R7022-R7023, R7025-R7459), says a 1425 cipher "had been
reconstructed by Pietro Domenico Gabbrielli (1863) and is printed in Meister (1902)", cites
Meister pp. 42-50 for Florence and "Crittografia Fiorentina by Pietro Gabrielli (archivist)
(around 1865), Archivio Secreto, Firenze (cited by Mark Knowles)". No Acciaioli 1512-1514 key and
no transcription of no. 120.

Archivio di Stato di Firenze site (through the Italian exit):

- Inventory N/9 (`n9_inventario.pdf`, indice, avvertenze, presentazione): gives the Dieci di
  Balia, Responsive filze and dates used above. No mention of cifre, alfabeti or Gabbrielli.
- Manoscritti inventory N/187: only unrelated cipher items.
- Online inventory index pages (D, C, M, S, tutti): no entry for cifre or Gabbrielli.
- "Archivi digitalizzati" portal: fondi list and free-text search for "cifre" and "cifra". The
  Dieci di Balia and the key volumes are not digitized there.
- `archivi_antiche_segnature.pdf`, `guida_siasfi.pdf`: nothing relevant.
- Quaderni della Rassegna degli Archivi di Stato 1 (1959 inventory of Signori, Dieci, Otto,
  Legazioni e commissarie): nothing on the key volumes.

Failed or empty: SIAS pages (sias-archivi.cultura.gov.it) redirected to a portal home page;
dspace.ut.ee (Marco Vito, HistoCrypt 2025 paper on a Florentine cipher) timed out; Internet
Archive metadata search for "lettere in cifra" with Gabbrielli or "Dieci di Balia" returned 0;
web searches for the Gabbrielli title returned nothing. The Ilardi guide attached to a
voynich.ninja thread needs a forum login. Not checked: HathiTrust, Meister 1902 (it prints early
Gabbrielli reconstructions; a 1512 key is unlikely there), Cerioni.

## How to obtain it

1. DECODE: log in and open record 5806 (also 5775 and 5797). Fastest route.
2. Yale: ask the Sterling Memorial Library microform staff for a scan of the frames of Ilardi
   reel 58 showing keys no. 89, 111 and 120 of Gabbrielli's volume. The Dataverse depositor and
   contact is Claire Bowern (Yale), who scanned nos. 1-47 from the same reel.
3. ASFi: request reproduction of "Alfabeti che servono a spiegare le lettere in cifra del
   carteggio dei Dieci di Balia dal 1424 al 1530, fatti dall'abate Pietro Domenico Gabbrielli
   (1862), volume I, alfabeti n. 89, 111 e 120 (Acciaioli Roberto)", noting that it was filmed for
   the Ilardi collection as "Chiavi delle cifre delle lettere ai Dieci di Balia, volume n. 1", and
   asking for the current segnatura.

Local files: everything fetched is under `images/gabbrielli/` (index crops `idx2_top.png`,
`idx2_bot.png`; inventories and the Ilardi guide text under `images/gabbrielli/lit/`).
