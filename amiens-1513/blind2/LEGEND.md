# Shape legend for the second blind pass (names only, no values)

Each crop shows one target line of cipher across its vertical middle, with its small superscript figures directly above it.
Descenders of the previous line show at the very top, and the superscripts and tops of the next line show along the bottom
edge: ignore both. The target line is the only one whose signs are complete from top to bottom. Files `R05_a.png` .. `R05_d.png` are the four quarters of line R05, left to right, overlapping by a few signs.

Write one token per sign: `shape` or `shape^superscript`.

**Superscripts** (the small figure above a sign; most signs have one, some have none):
- `1` a short straight vertical tick, like `ı` or `'`.
- `2` a flat wavy stroke, like `~`.
- `3` a small 3.
- `4` a small open 4, often like a tiny `+` or `4`.
- `5` a small slanted s or lightning-like hook, like `ʃ` or `s`. It is taller and more curved than the tick of `1`.
1 and 5 are the pair most often confused: look for a curve or double bend (5) against a single straight stroke (1). If you
cannot tell, write `{sign^1/sign^5}`. A superscript sits above its sign or slightly to its right. If it sits between two
signs write it on the left one and add nothing else.

**Shapes on the line**
- Figures as drawn: `2` (a z-like flat 2), `3`, `4` (open 4), `5` (small s-like 5), `7` (small flat-topped hook), `8` (closed 8), `0` (small closed circle).
- `6` = a 6 with a tall ascender swept to the right. `s6` = a low sigma-like sign, `σ`, with a flat top stroke to the right and no ascender.
- `9` = q-shape with a straight descender. `g` = the same but with a tail that hooks or curls to the left at the bottom.
- `x` (cross), `y` (y with a long tail), `t` (small barred t), `c` (small c), `O` (reversed c, `ɔ`), `p` (p with descender), `n` (small π-like sign), `+` (plain cross).
- `d` = small round d, its ascender curling back to the left. `G` = larger closed loop like `δ` or `ð` with the top stroke leaning right.
- `r` = small tick with a hooked top, like a little v on a stem. `T` = tall gallows, like `Γ` or a long-s with a flat top.
- `S` = large angular S, twice the size of `5`. `A` = triangle. `B`, `D` (square D), `M` (a "2h"-like ligature), `F` (tall barred F with a looped foot), `E`.
- `h` = tall 3 with an ascender. `Q` = `ß`-like sign. `alpha` = a large looped sign with an x-shaped crossing tail. `qu` = a Q-loop with a long flourish running right.
- `W` = a 6 or b with a short bar across its ascender. `w` = a small low box-like or ω-like sign.
- `Y` = a small 8 with an open or forked top, like `ɣ`.
- `phi` = a circle or oval cut by a vertical stroke that continues below the line. `slash0` = a circle cut by a slanting stroke that does not descend. `Z` = a short dash joined to a small 2 or loop. These three are easily confused: say which you see, or `{phi/slash0}`.
- `J` = long s with one or two bars across its foot. `H` = a tall 4-like stroke joined to a crossed pair, or two tall strokes crossed by a bar. `t#` = two short t's joined by one bar. `n#` = an m/n-like sign with a stroke through it. `ff`.
- A sign with a small letter or circle raised beside it: write `D^n`, `D^e`, `D^1`, `R^0`, `B^1`, `V^0`, `L^r`, `T^m`, `M^0` as you see it. `R` = an R-like sign with a long foot stroke. `V` = a large V. `K` = a capital-H-like sign.
- Two-figure numbers standing together (10, 12, 14, 15, 20, 22, 26 ...) are written as the number.
- `[del]` struck or blotted sign, `?` unidentifiable, `{a/b}` torn between two.

Do not try to make words. Record shapes and superscripts only.
