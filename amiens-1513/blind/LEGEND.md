# Shape legend for the blind transcription (names only, no values)

One token per sign, separated by spaces: `shape` or `shape^superscript`. The superscript is the small figure written
above a sign: 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5. In this hand the superscript **2 is usually a flat tilde-like stroke (~)**, 1 is a short
vertical tick, 5 looks like a small s or a hook, 4 is a small open 4, 3 a small 3. Superscripts often sit between two
signs; give it to the sign it is most nearly above. A sign with no superscript is written bare.

Shapes on the line (large signs):
- Figures as written: `2` (a 2 or a flat z-like 2), `3`, `4`, `5` (small s-like 5), `6`, `7` (small hook like a 7 or ˥), `8`, `9` (q-like, with a descender), `0` (small closed o).
- `x` (a cross of two strokes, like x or χ), `y` (y with a long tail), `t` (small barred t), `d` (round open uncial d, its top curling left), `G` (closed delta δ/ð with a loop and a top stroke leaning right, larger than d), `g` (g/9-like with a closed bowl and a hooked tail),
  `r` (small r-like or v-like tick, short), `c` (small c), `O` (reversed c, ɔ), `p` (p-like with a descender), `n` (n- or π-like small sign with two legs), `+` (a plain cross),
  `S` (large S), `A` (triangle Δ), `B` (capital B), `M` (large M-like sign, often like "2M" or "zh" joined), `D` (square capital D), `F` (tall barred F with a looped foot, descends below the line),
  `T` (T with a bar), `E` (E-like), `V` (large V or looped v).
- `W` = tall b-like sign with a hooked or flagged top (like "b" or "ƅ" with a small flourish). `w` = small box-like/ω-like sign (like a tiny "ϖ" or "ꙍ").
- `h` = tall sign like a narrow "h" or a tall "3"/"ʒ" with ascender (looks like "ɦ" or "ʅ").
- `Q` = β/ß-like sign (a tall stroke with two bowls). `α` = a large looped Q/α-like sign with a long tail crossing to the right (like a cursive "Q" or "ℒ").
- `X` = φ-like sign (circle or oval cut by a vertical stroke). `Z` = a horizontal dash joined to a small 2 or loop ("-2", "⌐o").
- `J` = long ʃ with one or two cross bars and a curled foot (descends well below the line). `R` = an "R"/"℞"-like or "IR"-like sign with a long foot stroke to the right.
- `H` = two tall strokes crossed by a bar (like "ll" struck through, or "tt" tall). `t#` = two small t's crossed by one bar (short). `n#` = an n/m-like sign crossed by a double stroke. `ff` = doubled f.
- `L^r` = a square L or "v" with a small raised r/re. `D^n`, `D^e` = D with a small raised n or e. `T^m` = T with a raised m. `qu` = a "Qu"/"Q4"-like ligature at line ends.
- Numbers written as plain numerals inside the line (10, 12, 14, 20, 22 ...) are written as the number; numbers between points as `.15.`.
- `[del]` = a struck-through or blotted sign. `?` = cannot identify. `{a/b}` = doubtful between two shapes, e.g. `{d/G}^3`.

Do not try to make words. Record shapes and superscripts only.
