# Second-model review of Acciaiuoli, doc. 192

20 November 2026. Reviewed commit `3b0ab4d` on `research/amiens-1513` (PR 41). Coordinates below are manuscript line and **one-based token index in the committed transcription**, including struck tokens. Several proposed rereadings would change those indices. I ran both requested commands, inspected their inputs and compared fresh Pillow enlargements of both faces of 192 and both leaves of Gabbrielli 120. The following are proposed editorial decisions. The transcription, key and tracker have not been amended.

1. **The word count does not support “solved.” High confidence.** The target README counts roughly 722 outright words and 33 editorially corrected words in its numerator. Even on its own division, 722/782 is 92.3%. Cyphersolver's rule dated 19 November 2026 counts only sure cipher words given by the key or parallel plaintext. A probable word, an unresolved code and a word with even one supplied letter count as holes. Its one-digit exception requires a surviving period key and other conditions that do not apply here. The older Pandolfini reports give *certain + probable* as a reading-by-sense statistic. These are different measures. [Cyphersolver rules at the reviewed revision](https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver/blob/81f14a3b7712397024f62658effdee077a39c84d/README.md#L1064-L1126) · [Pandolfini doc. 307](https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver/blob/81f14a3b7712397024f62658effdee077a39c84d/targets/pandolfini1514/reading_307.md)

   I recounted the **cipher body only** in `192-reading.txt`. Follow its whitespace (including separately spaced elisions), join end-of-line hyphens, count each printed code, numeral and joined `SuaMaestà` as one unit. Substitute each bracketed gloss for its preceding angle group. Give an unexpanded gap, including the ellipsis before *potevono?*, one provisional word. A word spanning a partial gap counts once. With mutually exclusive categories the count is **757 slots**: 649 without a bracket, unresolved portion, selected alternative or TSV repair; 12 further words touching a selected alternative; 20 further words touching a TSV repair; 49 with unqueried bracketed material; 6 with queried material; 18 standalone holes; 3 partially unresolved words (*offe‹e›so*, *c‹o x›*, *desidera‹n›*). Thus even crediting all 12 alternatives and 20 repairs gives only **681/757 (90.0%)** before admitting brackets. Reaching 95% on this provisional division requires 720 eligible words. The 649/757 (85.7%) is a markup count, itself generous as a count of sure words because it includes both occurrences of unvalued 15. Counting all 49 unqueried bracketed words would give 730/757 (96.4%). An omitted question mark cannot establish the probability of *costà*, *sono* or *d'esser*. The true extent of several holes, especially V13 and V14–15, is uncertain.

   **Proposed change.** Use `partial — substantially deciphered, with a continuous proposed reading` in TARGETS and the summaries. Give a word-by-word count with explicit extents or “extent unknown” for the holes. Keep a separate count of editorially recoverable sense if desired. Several image-supported corrections below would improve the strict numerator. They do not validate the present 96%.

2. **The 91.1% measures agreement with the editor's markup. High confidence.** Both `uv run python amiens-1513/verify.py` and `uv run python amiens-1513/blind_compare.py` exit 0. The first reproduces 1,894 tokens, four marked struck, 1,722 accepted and 168 unread out of 1,890. It verifies the committed literal stream **after** applying the 24 local overrides. It compares rows after removing square brackets, spaces, punctuation, accents and case, folding u/v and i/j, and retaining the letters *inside* angle brackets. The editor's placement of the angle brackets determines the coverage. A wholly speculative gloss can be replaced inside square brackets without failing the check. R06:19 and R08:20 both return `#15` and count as read. Changing the code's Italian meaning in the key would leave both verifiers green. Conversely even *mi* at R12:20 falls inside a group scored unread. Fourteen accepted signs carry alternatives and 24 are repairs. The reported 195 changes are a sequence-alignment difference against a retyped first pass, including changes of tokenization or naming. It is not a count of 195 independently corrected errors.

   **Proposed change.** Describe 91.1% as the share of unstruck **transcription tokens accepted into the proposed reading**, with these qualifications. Keep raw key output, repaired output and editorial reading distinguishable. Grade the sign identification, source of the value and semantic word separately. The English, word divisions, word count and palaeography have no automatic check here.

3. **The reported provenance totals contain an actual dispatch error. High confidence.** `verify.key_source()` looks up *every* string of digits as a code or numeral. `decode.value()` looks up an undotted single digit as a bare letter. Among accepted `key` tokens, 19 bare `0=c` and 15 bare `3=a` go to “this letter” despite their G120 rows. Four bare `8=t`, one `7=r` and one `5=m` go there despite their v6 rows. Two bare `2=o` and three `4=g` instead receive the G120 source of the pope or king. Examples include R01:6, R09:4, R03:39, V08:38 and R17:17. Using the actual decoding branch gives **1,611 G120 / 13 v6 / 48 pair / 12 this letter**, in place of **1,582 / 2 / 48 / 52**, before any palaeographic regrading. The checksum on `counts.json` reproduces the error.

   **Proposed change.** Have the decoder return the row(s) it used and emit the evidentiary grade per occurrence. `key`, `alt` and `repair` are processing statuses. H in CONVENTIONS §1 calls for a primary key source. G120 is an independent nineteenth-century reconstruction for this correspondent. Credit exact lookups explicitly as “attested in Gabbrielli 120 (reconstructed)”. Reserve unqualified period-key H for a documented period table. Use C where an identified sign is fixed by the contemporary clear, I for editorial supplies and M for unresolved alternatives. A value chosen by sense within 192 does not gain independent attestation by being stored in a TSV.

4. **The blind comparison overcredits agreement with the printed value. High confidence.** The second script reproduces 1,501/1,722 “strict”, 1,556/1,722 allowing aliases, and nine without an aligned counterpart. There is **one first-round witness per face**. Alignment is optimized using the aliases even for the strict total. `strict()` accepts any value of either transcription alternative, or the editor's repaired value. At R02:46 the blind `2^2` gives *co* but scores for the printed *bo*. At R02:49 bare `8` gives *t* but scores for numeral 8. At V12:1 blind `x` gives *l* but scores for the losing alternative of the printed *c*. On the **same alignment**, comparing only with the published value yields **1,476/1,722 (85.7%)** allowing all blind alternatives, **1,444/1,722 (83.9%)** taking the first blind alternative under the script's token conventions. Of the 25 extra strict hits, 22 come from repairs and three from losing alternatives. Both recalculations still include incorporated blind readings. The wide aliases (for instance, any of `d/G/0/W` for bare sigma) are agreements of possible shape naming.

   The script never opens `blind2/passA.txt` or `passB.txt`. Both give `n` at R04:49, against final `n#`; both give `7^5` at R05:8, against `r^5`; both give `t^2` at V08:24, against `t^5`; both give `7^4` at V16:32, against `r^4`. These local counterexamples matter even allowing for insertion/deletion alignment. V01:25 is split as `n t#` in A and is an alternative in B. For R02 *Borbona*, R08 *Martelli*, V03 *Cales* and V12 *Tucti*, the first-round files support recognizable shapes with repairs, renamed doubles, aliases or another vowel. They do not independently decode all four words verbatim. For instance, V12:19 is blind `8^4` (*te*) against `8^3` (*ti*). V18:28–30 genuinely gives *credo* even in the first-round file.

   **Proposed change.** Report first-choice letters, alternative-compatible letters and shape agreement separately. Identify borrowed coordinates and the actual witness at each disputed passage. Withdraw the claims that all two-reader agreements were adopted and that thirteen whole lines rest on two blind readers alone. The 190/191 calibration is helpful external evidence for values, but 607/661 on held-out signs includes cipher-assisted recognition of the clear. The smaller pre-read subset gives 78/93 first-choice and 87/93 best-alternative. Neither is a word-accuracy measurement on 192.

**Verdict.** I would publish a revised edition as a substantial partial reading of both faces. The Bourbon orders, force sizes, Martelli report, king's complaint, English embarkation, All Saints and closing prayer survive image checks. “Solved” and “about 96% of words” overstate the present evidence. I checked the two key leaves and the specified passages at the archive's native 1272-pixel page width, with additional crops for the findings above. This is a spot review of 192, not an independent transcription of all 1,890 signs. I could not settle every superscript, cancellation, missing word boundary, numeral referent or personal name. I read the 190/191 calibration and alignment evidence but did not independently transcribe the sibling manuscripts or verify the Sanudo and Desjardins passages, the completeness of the archival search, or how the original blind sessions were isolated. Those limits chiefly affect the historical identifications and the exact extent of the reading.
