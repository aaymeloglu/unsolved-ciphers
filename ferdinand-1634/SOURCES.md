# Source and further-evidence audit

Checked 23 September 2026. **No matching historical key or second witness of R1887 was found in this bounded search.** No additional plaintext is confirmed by these external checks. The proposed key, literal output and conjecture labels are unchanged.

## Historical keys

Selected images were inspected from all 17 DECODE Brussels SEA inventory 1 key records, **R937–R953**. All 40 attached images were downloaded with permission, but not every page was read. This does not establish that DECODE covers every key in the physical archival unit.

Several candidates have explicit conflicts with the proposed R1887 assignments:

| Record and inspected image | Candidate assignment | R1887 reconstruction |
|---|---|---|
| [R940](https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/940), I4941 P1 | `9=u` | `9=e` |
| [R945](https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/945), I4953 P1 | `10=ab` | `10=u` |
| [R947](https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/947), I4958 P1 | `43=e`, `46=m` | `43=in`, `46=pa` |
| [R950](https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/950), I4968 P1 | `12=b`, `16=f` | `12=pe`, `16=r` |
| [R953](https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/953), I4975 P1 and AP transcription | `16=B`, `43=Y`, word-code `ub=Petersberg` | `16=r`, `43=in`, `ub=mu` |

These conflicts reject the displayed tables as unchanged matches; they do not independently validate every R1887 assignment. R944 supplies a word-list fragment and cover, insufficient to exclude an absent alphabet. R952's three-letter groups encode whole words, including `tin=Wormbs` and `tun=Zeitung`, rather than the target's repeated internal syllables. The [AP transcription of R953](../ferdinand-1635-1640/sources/R953-DECODE-rejected-comparison.txt) retains its attribution.

**38 Vienna candidates** were screened using catalogue dates and language/symbol metadata. Ten received image and/or attached-transcription examination: R1193, R1207, R1218, R1260, R1290, R1292, R1306, R1538, R1540 and R1556. The other 28 were screened only at metadata level and are not conclusively excluded. The selection is not a complete survey of Vienna keys.

For example, [R1540](https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/1540), I6884 P1, and the closely related R1538 have `16=nn`, `9=x`, and whole-word `ub=Dominium Venetum`, conflicting with target r, e and mu. They are related tables, not independent cipher families. R1218 has a marked numerical syllabary, not the target's three-letter families; its diacritics must not be discarded when comparing numbers. None of these examinations supplied a usable matching key. Private photographs and derived images are not redistributed.

## Independent context, not a plaintext witness

Cuvelier and Lefèvre, *Correspondance de la Cour d'Espagne*, [volume III](https://www.heuristiek.ugent.be/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/CCE.3.pdf), printed p.21 no.66, summarizes the Infante's letter to Philip IV of **17 October 1634**: he proceeded toward the Netherlands despite requests from the King of Hungary and had reached the Rhine. Its archival reference is **Secrétairerie d'État et de Guerre 211, fol.81**. This independently supports the military setting of competing priorities. It does not confirm a particular cipher value, proposed word or missing clause. The original letter was not inspected.

The relevant chronological sections of volumes III and [VI](https://www.heuristiek.ugent.be/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/CCE.6.OCR_.pdf) did not supply the 28 October letter. Their coverage does not encompass all correspondence between the two Ferdinands. A same-date Stuttgart letter to the Emperor, Bierther BA II/10/2 no.82, concerns religious concessions and is a different document; the edition's manuscript source note was not obtained. These checks do not establish publication priority.

## Archival leads and their limits

The official [SEA inventory](https://agatha.arch.be/data/ead/BE-A0510_000022_002527/annexes/BE-A0510_000022_002527_fre.ead.pdf), pp.79–80, describes **540** as Ferdinand II/III correspondence, 1633–1641, and **541** as correspondence with the same rulers, 1634–1641. Current ordering references are **510 - I 074 - 540/541**. The target is in 540, making its immediate neighbours and any associated copy or decipherment the most direct untested lead. Neither a second letter using this key nor a surviving decipherment has been established. Unit 541 is a broader lead based on correspondents and dates.

The [Manuscrits Divers inventory](https://agatha.arch.be/nl/data/ead/BE-A0510_000766_803289/#I7668032891151) lists current **1151**, ordering reference **510 - I 677 - 1151**, as nineteenth-century copies of Ferdinand–Infante correspondence, 7 October 1633–19 May 1640. The Dutch catalogue labels these **Spanish**. Inclusion of the Latin R1887 letter is unknown, so this is a speculative second-witness lead. Do not confuse current 1151 with old 1151 in the inventory's concordance.

Live item records for 540, 541 and current 1151 have no linked online digital objects (`data-bs-dao=false`); this does not rule out offline scans or other photographs. Their contents were not inspected. No archive inquiry was sent.

A matching key, related ciphertext or readable copy could test the remaining signs and constructions. These are possible routes to stronger evidence, not a claim that the reading cannot advance otherwise or that an archival request would probably resolve it.
